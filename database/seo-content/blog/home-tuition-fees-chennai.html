That band holds from Mylapore and T Nagar to Anna Nagar, Tambaram and the far end of the OMR. Each tutor sets their own fee and you see it before the first class, which is a free demo, so a Chennai family's real work is not guessing a rate. It is deciding how many hours the child needs and which tutors can reach the home.

This guide therefore starts with hours rather than rupees. It decodes the fee sentence, suggests weekly hours by stage, turns them into money at both ends of the band and shows how Chennai's rail lines shape your shortlist.

How do we turn hours into a weekly and a monthly figure?

The sum is simple: hourly fee × hours a week. For a month, multiply that weekly total by 4.33, because a calendar month holds slightly more than four weeks. Before you read a single profile, work it out at both ends of the band so you know the widest spread.

Does where you live in Chennai change what you pay?

Not the band. A quote mostly reflects the class, the board, the subject and how well the tutor knows that syllabus. Your address affects a different thing: the number of tutors able to travel to you after school, and therefore the number of quotes on the table. In Chennai, the nearest station decides much of that.

Along the MRTS and the suburban lines

The MRTS runs from Chennai Beach past Light House, Thirumayilai and Mandaveli to Thiruvanmiyur, Perungudi and Velachery, and since March 2026 on to Puzhuthivakkam and St Thomas Mount. The suburban line through Nungambakkam, Kodambakkam, Mambalam, Saidapet, Guindy and Chromepet to Tambaram, and the line from Chennai Central through Perambur, Villivakkam, Ambattur and Avadi, carry many tutors too. Live within an easy auto ride of one of these stations and tutors from anywhere on that line become realistic options. See the South Chennai guide and the West and North Chennai guide.

Along the Blue and Green Lines

The Metro Blue Line links Tondiarpet and Washermanpet in the north with Thousand Lights, Teynampet, Saidapet, Guindy and Nanganallur Road further south. The Green Line runs underground through Anna Nagar and Shenoy Nagar, then on through Koyambedu, Vadapalani and Ashok Nagar to St Thomas Mount. A tutor who lives near either line can reach homes a long way along it without touching the evening roads.

The OMR, the ECR and the growing suburbs

Beyond Perungudi, most of the OMR and all of the ECR have no rail station, and the metro lines planned for the corridor, Lines 3 and 5, are still under construction with no passenger service. In Thoraipakkam, Sholinganallur, Navalur, Kelambakkam, Neelankarai and Medavakkam, tutors come by road, so the shortlist leans on tutors who live close by, and online classes cover specialist subjects. The rate is not higher or lower for it. See the OMR and ECR guide.

Which weeks of the school year need a line in the budget?

  1. The first weeks of the year. Fix the weekly hours, then review them after the first school test instead of paying for more from day one.
  2. The run-up to school and board exams. Extra revision hours are common. Settle their rate at the start of the year and give them an end date, such as one added hour weekly for the final stretch, so the total stays known.
  3. Festival weeks and family travel. Streets around the temple in Mylapore, the bazaars of T Nagar and the temple streets of Nanganallur get crowded on festival days. Agree whether a class moves online, shifts to a morning or pauses.
  4. Once results are out. Choose between a break, a lighter single weekly class or an early start on the next class's work. Pausing for a few weeks costs nothing and often helps.

Home, online or both?

A home tutor suits younger children and any subject that needs hands-on checking of written work. Online works well for a second subject, for IB and IGCSE programmes, and for areas where few tutors live nearby. On the OMR and the ECR in particular, pairing a local home tutor for the main subject with an online tutor for a specialist one keeps the choice wide without extra travel. To weigh the two, read home tutor or online tutor.

What should we settle before the first paid month?

  • Do we pay at the start of each month or once the month's classes are done?
  • How much notice does either side give to cancel, and is a missed class made up?
  • If a class moves online on a day travel is disrupted, is the rate the same?
  • Are revision hours before exams charged at the usual rate?
  • What is the fee if a brother or sister joins the same class?
  • In a gated community, has the tutor been added as a regular visitor, so no class time is lost at the gate?

Frequently asked questions

How much does a home tutor cost in Chennai?

How do we work out a monthly tuition budget in Chennai?

Multiply the hourly fee by weekly hours, then by 4.33 for a month. At the two ends of the band, two hours a week comes to about ₹6,900 a month at ₹800 an hour and about ₹21,700 at ₹2,500. Your shortlist's actual quotes will fall between those figures.

Do Tamil Nadu State Board students pay less than CBSE or ICSE students?

Not as a rule. The same band applies to every board. What the board changes is which tutor fits, since depth in the exact syllabus matters. The class, the subject and any entrance goal such as JEE or NEET move a quote more than the board itself does.

Does living on the OMR or ECR make home tuition dearer?

No, the band is the same across Chennai. Much of the OMR and all of the ECR have no rail station, so tutors come by road and the shortlist leans on tutors who live nearby. Pairing a local home tutor with online classes for a specialist subject keeps the choice wide.

Should we wait for the new metro lines before choosing a tutor?

No. Metro Lines 3, 4 and 5 are under construction and carry no passengers, so plan around the MRTS, the suburban lines, the Blue and Green Lines and the roads you use now. A tutor who reaches you comfortably today is the one to book for this school year.

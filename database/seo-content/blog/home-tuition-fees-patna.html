What does one hour of home tuition buy?

An hourly rate pays for the tutor's time in your home, but a useful hour has three parts, and it is worth asking any tutor how they divide it:

  • Explaining: going back over the idea the class teacher covered, in the order your child needs, with questions along the way.
  • Practice under watch: the child works through problems while the tutor watches the method, which is where most mistakes are caught.
  • Checking and setting work: marking the last set of homework and leaving a short set for the days between visits.

A tutor near ₹800 and one near ₹2,500 both sell an hour; what usually differs is how well they know your child's class and board. At the demo, watch how the time is split as closely as you watch the explanation.

One tutor for two subjects, or two tutors?

Up to Class 8, one tutor often covers maths and science in one set of visits. From Class 9 each syllabus deepens, and a separate tutor for the weaker subject often makes sense. By Classes 11 and 12, physics, chemistry, maths and biology are each taught in depth, and a tutor who teaches one of them thoroughly is often a better use of the money than one who spreads across three.

Does BSEB, CBSE or ICSE change the rate?

Rarely on its own. Patna students study under the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB), the state board, and under CBSE and CISCE (ICSE and ISC), and some families look for IB or IGCSE help as well. A fee follows class, subject and the tutor's experience more than the name of the board. What the board changes is the experience to look for:

  • BSEB: ask which classes the tutor has taught under the state board recently, and whether they teach in Hindi, English or a mix, so the lessons match the medium your child studies in.
  • CBSE: ask which CBSE classes they have taught recently and how they use the board's sample papers.
  • ICSE and ISC: ask whether they have taught the council's syllabus for this class, and how they prepare students for its papers.
  • IB and IGCSE: these programmes sit toward the upper end of the band, and a specialist for IB HL can charge more.

If your child is moving between boards, for example from BSEB to CBSE for Class 11, set aside a few extra hours in the opening months for the change of textbooks and question style.

What should we settle before the first paid class?

  1. The weekly slot. Fix the day, start time and length, and keep them.
  2. Missed classes. Agree what happens when either side cancels, and how a missed hour is made up.
  3. Exam-season hours. Ask whether extra classes before unit tests, pre-boards or the boards are charged at the usual rate.
  4. When fees are paid. Settle whether payment is made at the start of each month or after the classes are held.
  5. A review date. Pick a point, such as the next unit test, to check whether the hours are right.

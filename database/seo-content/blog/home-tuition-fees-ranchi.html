The hourly rate is only half of the sum. The other half is how long the help is needed. Many Ranchi families hire a tutor for the whole school year when the real need is a short push on one chapter, and others book a short block when the child needs steady support until the board exam. Is your child's need a short block or a whole year?

Before comparing quotes, name the job. A tutor hired to fix fractions in Class 7 and a tutor hired to see a Class 12 student through physics until the boards are doing very different work, and the total cost of each depends far more on the number of weeks than on a small difference in the hourly rate.

When a short block is enough

  • The gap is specific. Your child can name the chapter or skill that went wrong, such as algebraic identities, balancing equations or map work.
  • The rest of the subject is fine. Class tests outside that chapter are steady.
  • There is a clear finish line. A unit test, a half-yearly paper or the start of the next chapter gives the block an end date.

When a whole-year plan pays for itself

  • The subject builds on itself. Maths and physics in Classes 9–12 carry each chapter into the next, so a gap in one term shows up again in the next.
  • A board exam is at the end. Class 10 and Class 12 reward regular revision more than a late rush.
  • An entrance exam runs alongside school. JEE and NEET preparation needs months of problem practice, not a few weeks.

A useful middle path is to start with a block and review at its end. If the tutor and your child are working well, extend it; if the gap is closed, stop, with nothing lost.

JAC, CBSE or ICSE: does the board move the rate?

Seldom by itself. In Ranchi, children follow the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), which is the state board, or CBSE, or the CISCE's ICSE and ISC, and a smaller group needs IB or IGCSE support. A tutor's quote mostly tracks the class, the subject and how much teaching they have done at that level. Use the board to decide which experience to probe:

  • JAC: which state-board classes has the tutor handled in the last year or two, and do they explain in Hindi, English or a blend that suits your child's exam medium?
  • CBSE: which CBSE classes have they taught of late, and when do they bring in the board's sample question papers?
  • ICSE and ISC: are they at home with the council's syllabus for this class, and how do they train students for its longer written answers?
  • IB and IGCSE: expect quotes toward the upper end of the band, more for IB HL; an online specialist is often the realistic choice in Ranchi.

Does where you live in Ranchi change the cost?

Location seldom changes the band. What it does change is the number of tutors who can reach your home for a fixed weekly slot, which decides how many quotes land on your shortlist. Ranchi has no metro, so every home visit is by road: two-wheeler, auto, e-rickshaw or cab. Rail helps only at the edges, through Ranchi Junction, Hatia, Argora and Namkon stations.

Which money questions should you ask a tutor?

Most disagreements about tuition fees come from things nobody said out loud. Ask these at or just after the demo, and write the answers down.

AskWhy it matters
Is the fee per hour, per session or per month?A session of an hour and a half at an hourly rate costs more than it first appears; a monthly fee needs a fixed number of sessions behind it.
What happens if either of us cancels?Agree how a missed class is made up, and how much notice each side gives.
Are extra classes before tests charged at the same rate?Exam-season hours can add up quickly in Class 10 and Class 12.
Is the fee paid at the start of the month or after classes are held?Knowing this avoids an awkward conversation at the end of the first month.
When shall we review progress?A review date, such as the next unit test, tells you whether to extend, reduce or stop the hours.

How much does a home tutor cost in Ranchi?

What does a six-week catch-up block cost?

Multiply the hours by the hourly fee. Two hours a week for six weeks is 12 hours, which comes to ₹9,600 at ₹800 an hour and ₹30,000 at ₹2,500 an hour. Review at the end of the block and extend it only if the gap is not yet closed.

Is a JAC tutor cheaper than a CBSE or ICSE tutor?

No fixed rule says so. The class, the subject and the tutor's experience at that level drive most quotes, and every tutor chooses their own rate. What the board changes is your checklist: for JAC, ask about recent state-board classes and whether lessons run in Hindi, English or a blend.

Does having no metro in Ranchi raise tuition fees?

No. The fee band is the same across the city. What travel by road changes is how many tutors can reach you for a regular slot. On the outer edges, where homes are spread out, adding an online tutor for a specialist subject widens the choice without changing the band.

Is there any cost before a Ranchi family picks a tutor?

None. You receive two or three matched tutors with their fees already visible, and each gives a free first class. Money changes hands only once you have picked one tutor and fixed a weekly slot with them, and moving to a different tutor later carries no charge on NXTutors.

What sets most of a quote in Hyderabad?

Four things explain most of the difference between two quotes for the same child:

  • Class. Primary and middle-school help usually costs less per hour than Classes 11 and 12, where each science subject needs deeper subject knowledge.
  • Board. Families here follow CBSE, ICSE/ISC, IB/IGCSE or the state board. In Telangana, the Board of Secondary Education runs the SSC examination at the end of Class 10, and the Telangana Board of Intermediate Education covers Classes 11 and 12, which students call Intermediate. IB and IGCSE sit toward the upper end of the band.
  • Goal. Keeping up with school work is one thing; JEE or NEET preparation alongside the board syllabus is another, and it sits toward the upper end.
  • The tutor's own experience with that board and class, which each tutor reflects in the fee they set.

Location mainly decides how many tutors can reach you, and so how many quotes you can compare.

Where does each stage tend to sit in the band?

  • Classes 1 to 5: usually the lower part of the band. Regular attendance and patience matter more than subject depth.
  • Classes 6 to 8: lower to middle. The move to algebra and separate sciences is when many families first add a subject tutor.
  • Class 10: middle of the band for CBSE, ICSE and the state SSC syllabus, with more hours as the board exam nears.
  • Classes 11 and 12, including Intermediate: toward the upper end, with a separate tutor for each science subject.
  • IB and IGCSE: toward the upper end at every stage, because the tutor needs to know the programme's assessment style well.
  • JEE and NEET: toward the upper end; specialists for JEE Advanced can charge more.

Does a Class 12 student need three separate tutors?

Often, yes, for physics, chemistry and maths or biology, since each needs depth at that level. Some tutors cover two related subjects well. Price six hours a week at both ends of the band, then decide which subject needs the most time and which could be taken online.

Do state board students pay less than CBSE or ICSE students?

Not by rule. Each tutor sets their own fee, and class and goal matter more than board for CBSE, ICSE and the Telangana state syllabus. IB and IGCSE sit toward the upper end, as do Classes 11 and 12 and JEE or NEET preparation, whichever board the student follows.

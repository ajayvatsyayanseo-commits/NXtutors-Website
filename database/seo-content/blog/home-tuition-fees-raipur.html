In many households, one child is in middle school while an older brother or sister faces board exams. Three common arrangements put different hours into the week. They are planning examples, not quotes.

In practice the two children are rarely quoted the same rate. A Class 6 child's maths tutor usually sits nearer the lower end of the band, and a Class 12 physics or JEE tutor nearer the upper end, so plan each child's hours separately.

When one tutor for both children makes sense

Back-to-back visits save the tutor a second trip and often make a weekly slot easier to fix. They work when the subjects overlap, for example maths for both children, and when the tutor is comfortable at both levels. Ask at the demo how they would split the time.

When two tutors are the better spend

If the older child needs chemistry or biology at Class 11–12 depth and the younger needs reading and English support, one person rarely teaches both well. Two tutors cost more in hours but put each hour where it is most useful.

What should a tutor's fee message spell out?

Before the first paid class, ask each tutor to put their terms in one message. A clear message covers:

  1. The rate and its unit. Whether the figure is hourly, for each class or for the month, and if monthly, how many classes it pays for.
  2. The length of a class. The fee follows the length, so agree how long each class runs.
  3. Siblings. Whether a second child in the same visit is charged at the same rate.
  4. Missed classes. How much warning the family and the tutor each give before cancelling, and when a lost class is made up.
  5. Online switches. Whether an evening switched to an online class is billed like a home visit.
  6. A review point. The half-yearly exams or the end of a term, when you decide whether to keep, trim or stop the hours.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a home tutor cost in Raipur?

How should we plan tuition for two children in the same home?

Plan each child's hours separately, because a Class 6 subject and a Class 12 science subject are rarely quoted the same rate. One tutor for both, back to back, saves a trip when the subjects overlap. Ask whether the second child's hour is charged at the same rate.

Is a CGBSE tutor cheaper than a CBSE tutor?

Not in general. Quotes depend mostly on the class, the subject and how long the tutor has taught at that level, and each tutor fixes their own figure. What the board alters is your checklist: for CGBSE, confirm recent state-board teaching and that the tutor can work in your child's medium, Hindi or English.

Should we keep paying the tutor during the summer break?

Only if you agree a plan for it. Some families drop to one class a week to repair a weak chapter; others pause completely. If you pause, ask whether the tutor will hold your weekly slot for the new session, and how much notice they need before classes restart.

Does home tuition cost more in Saddu or Kamal Vihar?

No. Saddu and Kamal Vihar sit in the same fee band as the rest of Raipur. Because both are still growing, fewer tutors may live near enough for a fixed weekly time, so expect a shorter home list. Pairing it with an online tutor for a specialist subject gives more choice at the same rates.

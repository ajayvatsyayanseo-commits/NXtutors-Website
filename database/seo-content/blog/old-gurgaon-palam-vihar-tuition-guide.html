Old Gurgaon and Palam Vihar are the settled heart of the city. Most homes here are independent houses and builder floors in plotted sectors and older colonies, often with extended families living together, and many tutors have lived and taught in the same few sectors for years. Arranging tuition is simpler in some ways than in the new towers, but the busy market roads, narrow lanes and afternoon school timings bring their own questions.

This guide covers the areas in this zone of Gurgaon (Gurugram), how home tuition typically runs in plotted sectors, the boards and classes families here usually ask about, when online is worth considering, and a practical checklist for booking a demo. It also looks at a common situation in these homes: tuition for two or more children, or cousins, under one roof.

How home tuition works in this zone

Plotted sectors and independent houses

Most tutors here simply ring the bell. There is no society app and usually no guard, which makes the first visit quick. It also means you are the only check on who comes to the door, so meet the tutor at the demo, keep an adult at home during classes, and read our home tutor safety checklist before you start.

Parking is the practical snag. Lanes in the older sectors are narrow and cars line both sides in the evening. Many tutors here travel by two-wheeler for this reason. If the tutor drives, tell them where they can stop without blocking a neighbour's gate.

Where to hold the class

In a busy multi-generation home, the right room matters as much as the right tutor. Pick a table away from the television and the main entrance, with good light, where a family member can pass by without interrupting. A fixed spot helps the child treat tuition as a routine.

Timings

Children in the older sectors often get home from school earlier than those in the far newer sectors, so afternoon classes are common and practical. The roads around the old city markets become slow in the evening, and a tutor crossing NH-48 from the other side of the city at peak time will struggle to be punctual. An afternoon or early-evening slot, with a tutor from within the zone, is usually the most reliable arrangement.

Travel from neighbouring zones

Many tutors in this zone live locally, and a good number have taught board classes here for a long time. Palam Vihar can also draw on tutors from the nearer Dwarka Expressway sectors. Tutors from the MG Road side can reach the eastern old sectors in the afternoon. Tutors from Sohna Road, Golf Course Extension Road or New Gurgaon are rarely a realistic weekday option.

Boards and classes families here commonly look for

CBSE is the most common board in this zone, and board years drive most requests: Class 10 Maths and Science, and Class 12 Physics, Chemistry and Maths, with some families also asking for Accountancy, Hindi or Sanskrit. JEE and NEET preparation alongside school is common from Class 11. There are ICSE and ISC families too, and a smaller number on international boards.

Because many children here share a home with siblings or cousins in nearby classes, parents often ask whether one tutor can teach two children. For Classes 6 to 10 this can work well, as separate back-to-back classes on the same visit. In senior classes, subject specialists usually serve each child better. Checklist for booking a demo in this zone

  • Give your sector or colony, and a landmark the tutor will recognise.
  • Tell the tutor where to park or suggest coming by two-wheeler.
  • Choose a quiet room and a fixed table before the demo.
  • If two children need tuition, say so upfront and ask about back-to-back classes.
  • Prefer an afternoon or early-evening demo slot, before the market traffic builds.
  • Ask the tutor which boards and classes they have taught recently, not just in total.
  • Share the latest school test and the school's exam calendar.
  • Keep an adult at home and use our demo class checklist to judge the class.

Frequently asked questions

Can one tutor teach both my children?

Often, yes, for Classes 6 to 10, as separate classes on the same visit. Tell us both classes and boards when you ask, so the shortlist includes tutors who cover both.

Is there an area page for Palam Vihar?

Not yet. Include Palam Vihar in your request and we match tutors who can reach it; the nearer Dwarka Expressway area pages also list tutors close to Palam Vihar.

Our house has no guard or gate. How do we keep tuition safe?

Meet the tutor at the demo, keep an adult at home during classes, hold the class in a shared room, and keep class messages on a parent's phone.

Are experienced board-class tutors easier to find here?

Many tutors in the older sectors have taught CBSE board classes locally for years. Still ask about recent experience with the current syllabus, and judge the demo on teaching, not on years alone.

When does online make more sense in Old Gurgaon?

Mainly for international boards or rare subjects where no suitable tutor is close by, and for extra revision sessions before exams. For regular board-year teaching, most families here prefer a home tutor.

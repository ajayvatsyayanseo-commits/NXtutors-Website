Sohna Road and the older colonies of Central Gurgaon (Gurugram) sit side by side, but they are very different places to arrange tuition. On one side are plotted colonies such as South City 1 and Sushant Lok 2 and 3, with independent floors and short internal roads. On the other are the townships and gated societies along Sohna Road, where a single evening traffic jam on the main road can decide whether a tutor arrives on time.

This guide covers the areas in these two zones, the one rule that matters most here (which side of Sohna Road the tutor lives on), what boards and classes families ask for, when online beats home tuition, and a practical checklist for booking a demo class. It leans towards board-exam years, because that is where most requests in this part of the city are focused.

How home tuition works in this zone

The Sohna Road rule

Sohna Road carries heavy traffic in the evening, and crossing it at the wrong time can add a long wait to a short journey. The simplest way to protect your class timings is to prefer a tutor who lives, or already teaches, on the same side of the road as you. It is often worth waiting a day or two for such a tutor rather than taking the first available one from across the road.

The same logic applies to the service lanes and the turns into the larger townships. Ask the tutor how they plan to come in, not just how far away they live.

Colonies, townships and societies

In South City 1 and Sushant Lok 2 and 3, most homes are independent houses or builder floors. There is no guard desk in most lanes, so entry is simple, but street parking in the evening can be tight. Tell the tutor where they can leave a car or a two-wheeler.

Nirvana Country, Malibu Towne and South City 2 are larger townships, often with a mix of villas, floors and low-rise blocks, and usually with a gate at each entrance. The guard may call you when a new visitor arrives. Some Sohna Road sectors have high-rise gated societies with app-based entry, which works like any other tower: pre-approve the tutor once and the rest is routine.

Timings

In the colonies of Central Gurgaon, school buses usually drop children home earlier than in the far newer sectors, so a 4:30 pm or 5 pm class is often realistic. On Sohna Road, aim to finish the class before the tutor's own journey home coincides with the worst of the evening traffic. For board-year students with school extra classes, weekend mornings are useful for a longer session.

Travel from neighbouring zones

Tutors from Golf Course Road and the DLF side can reach Central Gurgaon easily in the afternoon. Tutors from Golf Course Extension Road can reach the Sohna Road townships, provided they do not need to cross the main road at peak time. Tutors coming from New Gurgaon are usually not practical for weekday evenings here.

Boards and classes families here commonly look for

This part of the city leans towards CBSE and ICSE schools. Requests are strongest in the board years: Class 10 Maths and Science, Class 12 Physics, Chemistry and Maths, and ICSE Maths in Classes 9 and 10. JEE and NEET foundation from Class 9 is also a common request, usually as school support alongside coaching rather than a replacement for it.

For board years, the tutor's plan matters more than their location. Ask how they will cover the syllabus, when they will start past papers and timed practice, and how they will fit around school pre-boards. Checklist for booking a demo in this zone

  • Say which side of Sohna Road you are on, and name your colony, township or society.
  • Ask each tutor where they will travel from on class days, and whether they cross the main road.
  • For a township, tell the guard in advance, or pre-approve the tutor on the app.
  • For a floor, sort out parking before the tutor arrives.
  • In a board year, share the school's test calendar and the latest marked paper.
  • Ask the tutor for a written plan for the first month, including when past papers start.
  • Check that the tutor knows your board: CBSE and ICSE Maths are not taught the same way.

Frequently asked questions

Does it really matter which side of Sohna Road the tutor lives on?

For weekday evening classes, often yes. Crossing the road at peak time can make a tutor late for every class. For weekend mornings it matters much less.

Our township has a gate but no app. How do we manage entry?

Give the guard the tutor's name and phone number before the first class, and ask whether a regular visitor can be noted down. Most gates handle this routinely.

When should a Class 10 student start home tuition?

Ideally near the start of the school session, which begins in April in most schools, so the tutor can pace the syllabus and leave time for past papers. Starting later is still useful, but the plan becomes more about revision.

Can one tutor cover both Maths and Science?

For Classes 6 to 10, many tutors can. For Classes 11 and 12, a separate specialist for Physics, Chemistry or Maths is usually better.

Is ICSE tuition easy to find here?

Tutors for ICSE are available in this zone, but check that the tutor has taught the ICSE syllabus recently, as its content and answer style differ from CBSE.

What goes into a tutor's quote?

A quote is more than a number per hour. Each line below changes what you actually pay, and only some of them are set by the tutor alone.

Where does your child's stage sit in the band?

  • Classes 1–5: homework support and reading or number work usually sit nearer the lower end of the band.
  • Classes 6–8: one tutor often covers maths and science together, which keeps the number of visits down.
  • Classes 9–10: the syllabus deepens and many families move to a separate tutor for the weaker subject.
  • Classes 11–12: physics, chemistry, maths and biology each need depth, and quotes sit toward the upper end.
  • JEE and NEET: quotes climb toward the upper end of the band, and specialists for JEE Advanced can go past ₹2,500 an hour.
  • IB and IGCSE: toward the upper end, with IB HL specialists charging more; an online tutor widens the choice.

Does your side of the river change the quote?

Rarely the rate, but often the shortlist. Jamshedpur sits where the Kharkai and the Subarnarekha meet, and the rivers decide which tutors can reach you easily. Without a metro in the city, tutors arrive by road on a two-wheeler, by auto or by cab. The railway helps tutors who live further out, through Tatanagar, Adityapur, Gamharia, Salgajhari and Govindpur stations.

Does JAC, CBSE or ICSE change the quote?

Hardly ever by itself. Schools in Jamshedpur follow the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), the state's own board, as well as CBSE and the CISCE's ICSE and ISC, and a few families need IB or IGCSE help. A quote rises and falls mainly with the class, the subject and the depth of the tutor's teaching record. The board tells you what to check. With JAC, look for recent state-board teaching in the language your child writes answers in. With CBSE, look for recent classes at the same level and regular practice from official sample papers. With ICSE and ISC, look for comfort with the council's detailed written answers.

How do we compare two quotes with different session lengths?

Convert both to hours a week, then multiply by the hourly rate and by four for a rough month. Two sessions of an hour and a half give three hours a week, the same as three one-hour sessions. Once the hours match, compare the rate, then judge the teaching itself at the free demo.

Is home tuition dearer in Bistupur than in Mango or Adityapur?

Not as a rule. Tutors set their own fees and one band covers Bistupur, Mango and Adityapur alike. What your side of the river changes is which tutors can reach you easily for a regular slot, so a nearer tutor mainly saves travel time and keeps the start time steady.

Does a JAC tutor charge less than a CBSE tutor?

Usually the board makes little difference. The class, the subject and the depth of a tutor's teaching record move a quote far more. With a JAC student, check the tutor's recent state-board teaching and whether lessons will be in the language your child uses in the exam, Hindi or English.

2. Exam-Focused CBSE Class 10 Maths Tips

Start Early and Stay Consistent

Revise formulas daily.

Solve at least 20 problems every alternate day.

Time yourself while solving problems.

Chapter-Wise Practice

Real Numbers → Understand proofs and solve exercise problems.

Polynomials → Memorize identities, practice factorization.

Triangles → Practice constructions and theorems repeatedly.

Trigonometry → Use formulas regularly in application-based problems.

50% solving questions.

3. Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping Proofs
Geometry proofs form an important part of the paper. Practice them thoroughly.

Ignoring Formula Derivations
Students memorize formulas but fail to understand their derivation. This leads to mistakes in problem-solving.

Not Practicing Word Problems
Trigonometry and application-based problems require understanding of concepts rather than rote memorization.

Overconfidence in Easy Chapters
Easy chapters like Polynomials are often ignored but can carry significant marks.

Not Revising Multiple Times
One-time learning leads to forgetting. Revision schedules are critical.

Incorrect Units in Geometry Problems
Pay attention to units, especially in mensuration problems like surface area and volume.

Skipping PYQs
Many students practice randomly. Structured practice through PYQs improves accuracy and speed.

4. Solved Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

Example PYQ 1 – Quadratic Equations

Q: Solve the quadratic equation:
x² - 5x + 6 = 0
Solution:
The quadratic formula is x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a
Here, a = 1, b = -5, c = 6
x = [5 ± √(25 - 24)] / 2
x = [5 ± √1] / 2
x = [5 ± 1] / 2
So, x = 3 or x = 2

➡ Practice more quadratic equations by solving exercises 4.1, 4.2, and sample papers.

Example PYQ 2 – Trigonometry

Q: Find the value of sin²A + cos²A.
Solution:
sin²A + cos²A = 1
This identity is frequently tested in different forms.

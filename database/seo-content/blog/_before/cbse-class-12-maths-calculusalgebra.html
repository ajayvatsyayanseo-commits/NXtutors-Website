CBSE Class 12 Maths Tips – Smart Study Plan for Better Scores

Tip 1: Practice Daily

Mathematics is best learned through regular practice. Set aside at least 2 hours each day for problem-solving.

Tip 2: Revise Formulas Frequently

Create a formula sheet and revise it twice a week. Pay special attention to differentiation and integration formulas.

Tip 3: Solve Previous Year Questions

Past year questions give insight into frequently asked problems and help you understand how to approach them.

Tip 4: Work on Conceptual Problems

Instead of memorizing problems, focus on understanding concepts and applying formulas to new problems.

Tip 5: Time Your Practice

During mock tests, time yourself to simulate exam conditions. This improves speed and accuracy.

Tip 6: Don’t Skip Word Problems

Probability and maxima-minima problems often come in word format. Practice reading and solving them correctly.

Tip 7: Group Study

Discuss difficult problems with peers to gain multiple approaches to problem-solving.

Common Mistakes to Avoid in CBSE Class 12 Maths

Mistake 1: Rushing Through Derivations

Students often skip steps or try to memorize formulas without fully understanding them. This leads to mistakes in application problems.

Mistake 2: Ignoring Numerical Practice

Numerical problems require constant practice. Avoid skipping them as they are frequently asked in exams.

Mistake 3: Not Writing Definitions Clearly

In theory-based questions, a lack of clear definitions can lead to loss of marks. Practice writing them concisely and accurately.

Mistake 4: Misinterpreting Word Problems

Especially in probability and maxima-minima problems, misunderstanding the question can result in incorrect answers. Read questions carefully.

Mistake 5: Poor Time Management

Spending too much time on one problem leaves less time for others. Practice with a timer to manage time effectively.

Mistake 6: Overlooking Diagrams and Graphs

Many calculus problems require graphical understanding. Practice drawing graphs to support your solutions.

Mistake 7: Not Checking Units or Final Answers

Always review your final answer for units, signs, and significant figures.

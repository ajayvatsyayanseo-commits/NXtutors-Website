Three questions to settle before you compare quotes

Answer these first, and every tutor ends up pricing the same job.

  1. What is the tuition for? Keeping up with schoolwork, catching up on earlier gaps, preparing for the Class 10 or Class 12 board papers, or entrance preparation alongside Class 12. Each needs a different kind of tutor and a different amount of planning.
  2. How many hours a week, and for how long? A clear number of visits and a length for each visit turns an hourly rate into something you can plan around.
  3. At home, online or both? In Leh this question matters more than in most cities, because the answer may change between the summer term and the coldest months.

What a tutor needs from you to quote properly

A short, complete request saves a round of messages and gives a quote that matches the real work. Include:

  • Class and, for Classes 11–12, the stream. A Class 3 child learning to read fluently and a Class 12 student working through physics numericals are very different jobs.
  • The board. In Leh this is usually CBSE; if your child follows another syllabus, say so plainly.
  • Each subject by name. One tutor covering several junior subjects is a different arrangement from a specialist for one senior subject.
  • The area and a landmark. Say whether you live in the town, in Choglamsar or Spituk, or in one of the villages along the Indus, and name the cluster if your village has several.
  • The days and times that suit you, including whether a midday slot is possible in winter.

CBSE in Leh: what the board means for the match

CBSE's own affiliation list carries Ladakh as a separate entry, and most of the affiliated schools it lists for Leh district are government high and higher secondary schools, in Leh town and in places such as Housing Colony, Stok, Phyang, Thiksey and Chuchot. Ladakh's School Education Department manages the government schools, and CBSE's regional office at Mohali covers Ladakh.

For fees, this has a simple consequence. The board rarely moves a rate on its own; what it does is narrow the field. A tutor who knows the CBSE books, the chapter order and the way answers are marked can start teaching from the first visit. Reading a quote: per hour, per class or per month

Quotes reach families in different units, and two that look different can describe the same work. Before comparing, put each one into the same shape:

  • the unit (an hour, a class or a month);
  • the length of each class, as clock times rather than "one class";
  • the number of classes each week;
  • whether homework is given and marked between visits;
  • whether online and extra revision classes cost the same as regular ones.

If a quote gives a monthly sum but no count of classes, or talks of a "class" without saying how long it lasts, ask politely before you agree.

A message to send before the first paid class

A brief written message, answered by the tutor, does the job. Cover:

  1. How the fee is counted and when it is paid.
  2. The days, start time and length of each class.
  3. The address, with the lane, cluster or village name and a landmark the tutor can spot.
  4. Where a two-wheeler or car can stop near the house.
  5. How much notice each side gives to cancel, and how a missed class is made up.
  6. The winter plan: which classes move online or to midday, and from when.
  7. A review date, such as the first unit test, when you decide together whether to keep, add or reduce hours.

Do villages outside the town pay more for home tuition?

No. The same band applies in Choglamsar, Stok, Phyang or Thiksey as in the town. What differs is the number of tutors able to get to a given home for a regular slot. A tutor already teaching nearby is usually the steadiest choice, and online classes can cover any subject no local tutor offers.

Should winter classes cost the same as summer ones?

That is for you and the tutor to agree, and it is worth settling at the start. Many Leh families move to online or midday classes in the long winter break. Decide when the change begins, whether class length stays the same, and whether an online class carries the home-visit rate.

Which board should I mention in the request?

Name the board your child's school follows, which in Leh is usually CBSE, along with the class and, for Classes 11–12, the stream. The board rarely changes a rate, but it decides which tutor fits, because a tutor who knows the CBSE books and marking can start teaching straight away.

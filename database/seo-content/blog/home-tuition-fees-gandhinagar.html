Will office hours shape your class slot?

Gandhinagar runs to a working day: state offices in Sector 16 and around the secretariat, the industrial estate in Sector 26, IT offices in Infocity and the business district at GIFT City. Roads are slowest as those offices open and close. Traffic does not change a rate, but late starts waste paid hours. In most of the city a late-afternoon or weekend slot runs more smoothly than one at the close of the working day, and in GIFT City an online class on busy weekdays suits many families. Agree one rule in advance: if a class starts late because of traffic, does it run its full length or finish at the usual time?

GSEB, CBSE, ICSE or IB: does the board change the quote?

Rarely by itself. Gandhinagar children study under the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB), which is the state board, under CBSE or under the CISCE (ICSE and ISC); fewer follow IB or IGCSE. The board mainly changes what you should check.

  • GSEB: tell us the medium your child studies in, and ask whether the tutor can explain in it and knows the prescribed textbooks. For exam notices and timetables, rely on the board's own website, gseb.org. See Gujarat Board tutors in Gandhinagar.
  • CBSE: ask which CBSE years the tutor knows well and when sample papers enter the plan. See CBSE home tutors in Gandhinagar.
  • ICSE and ISC: check the tutor knows the CISCE syllabus for this year and drills neatly set-out long answers. See ICSE tutors in Gandhinagar.
  • IB and IGCSE: expect the upper part of the band, more for IB HL; online tutors for Gandhinagar students widen the choice. Ask whether a tutor's online rate differs from their home rate.

What should go into your written fee note?

Before the first paid class, write down the tutor's answers to these.

  1. The basis of the fee: hourly, per lesson or monthly; if monthly, how many classes it covers and when it is paid.
  2. Length and start time: a longer lesson billed by the hour costs more per visit than it first seems.
  3. Late starts and cancellations: whether a delayed lesson runs full length, the notice each side owes, and how lost lessons are replaced.
  4. Online switches: when a class moves online and whether the fee changes.
  5. Exam season: whether extra revision classes are at the usual rate, and how many are planned.
  6. A review point: the next unit test or the end of a term, when you decide whether the hours continue, shrink or end.

Do tutors charge more in GIFT City than in the sectors?

No. One band applies across Gandhinagar, and each tutor sets their own rate whatever the address. What differs is practical: GIFT City towers have controlled entry and weekday office traffic is heavy, so register the tutor at the tower's entrance and consider evening, weekend or online classes.

Is a GSEB tutor cheaper than a CBSE or ICSE tutor?

Not by default. The class, the subject and how often the tutor has taught that level decide most quotes. For a GSEB student the more useful check is whether the tutor can teach in your child's medium and knows the prescribed textbooks, and dates and notices should come from the board's official website.

How many hours of tuition does a Class 10 student need?

A common starting point is two classes a week for each subject that is genuinely weak, with a few extra classes before the board exams. Review after two unit tests: add hours if marks keep slipping, and trim them once marks hold steady across a term. The hours matter more than the rate.

What happens if the tutor arrives late because of traffic?

That depends on what you agree at the start, so settle it before the first paid class. Many families ask the tutor to run the full class when the delay is short, or to make up the time later in the week. Choosing a slot outside office hours prevents most late starts.

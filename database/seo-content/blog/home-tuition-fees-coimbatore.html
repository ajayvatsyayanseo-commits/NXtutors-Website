What will the first eight weeks cost, step by step?

Most of the money decisions happen early. Here is the usual sequence, with the point at which each rupee is committed.

  1. The request. You give the class, board, subjects, your neighbourhood and a budget. Nothing is paid, and nobody above your budget is suggested.
  2. The shortlist. Two or three matched tutors arrive, each with a fee on the profile. Compare them before you book anything.
  3. The demo. The first class with the tutor you choose is free. Judge the teaching on a chapter your child is studying now.
  4. Weeks one to four. The first paid month. Keep the hours modest; two visits a week for one subject is a sensible start.
  5. The first school test. Use the result to decide whether the hours should go up, stay the same or come down.
  6. Weeks five to eight. If the fit is wrong, switching tutor is free, so there is no reason to keep paying for lessons that are not working.

Putting the review at the first school test, rather than at the start, keeps a family from paying for hours it may not need.

Does your part of Coimbatore change the fee?

Not the band. What your location changes is how many tutors can reach you on a school evening, and so how many quotes you get to compare. In Coimbatore that depends mostly on the main road you live on, since there is no metro running; one has been proposed but is not sanctioned.

Home, online or a split: three ways to use the same budget

  • All at home. Suits younger children and subjects where the tutor needs to watch the working, such as Maths and Chemistry numericals.
  • Home for the main subject, online for the second. A nearby tutor visits for the subject that needs the most help, and an online tutor takes a second subject. This works well in Vadavalli, Kovaipudur and the northern end of Sathy Road, where fewer tutors live close.
  • One tutor, both ways. A weekly home visit plus a short online session for doubts before a test, with the same tutor for both.

Which costs are easy to miss?

  • Extra revision before examinations. Many families add hours before school tests and board papers. Agree the rate for these at the start and give them an end date.
  • A second child. If a brother or sister joins the visit, ask what the fee becomes before the first joint class, not after.
  • Festival weeks and travel. Around Pongal, Deepavali and family holidays, decide whether a class moves online, shifts to a morning or pauses. A pause should cost nothing.
  • Missed classes. Settle who gives notice, how much, and whether a missed class is made up.

What goes into a one-page fee note?

A short written note, even a message both sides keep, prevents most disagreements. It should say:

  • the fee per hour, and the length of each visit;
  • whether payment is at the start of each month or after the month's classes;
  • the notice period for cancelling a class, on either side;
  • whether an online class on a day of travel disruption costs the same;
  • the rate and end date for any extra revision hours;
  • for a gated complex, that the tutor has been added as a regular visitor, so no class time is lost at the entrance.

What does a visit of an hour and a half cost?

Is Tamil Nadu State Board tuition cheaper than CBSE or ICSE tuition?

Not as a rule. The same band covers every board in Coimbatore. The board decides which tutor fits, because depth in that exact syllabus matters, while the class, the subject and any entrance goal such as JEE or NEET move a quote more than the board itself.

Does living in Saravanampatti or on Avinashi Road make tuition dearer?

No, there is no separate rate for any part of the city. Location changes how many tutors can reach you after school. On Sathy Road and Avinashi Road, a tutor who lives on your side of the road keeps better time, and online lessons can add a specialist subject.

Should we wait for a Coimbatore metro before choosing a tutor?

No. A metro has been proposed for Coimbatore but is not sanctioned, so plan around the buses, local trains and roads you use today. A tutor who reaches your home comfortably this term, on your own side of the main road, is the one to book for this school year.

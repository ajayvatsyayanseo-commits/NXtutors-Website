Why a ten-week term is a useful unit

Ten weeks is long enough to work through a block of chapters and short enough to review honestly. At the end, look at a unit test and decide whether to keep the hours, trim them or stop. Paying monthly is normal; planning by the term stops hours creeping upward without a decision.

What happens to the bill in winter and the monsoon?

Weather seldom changes a tutor's rate, but it does change how many classes run as planned, and an unplanned cancellation is where both money and learning leak away. Two seasons matter in Dehradun. In winter, evenings turn cold and dark early, most of all on the upper valley around Jakhan, Rajpur and Malsi. In the monsoon, heavy showers can slow travel right across the city.

Uttarakhand Board, CBSE, ICSE or IB: does the board move the quote?

Rarely on its own. Children in Dehradun study under the Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE), which is the state board, or under CBSE, or the CISCE's ICSE and ISC, and a smaller group needs IB or IGCSE support. A quote mostly follows the class, the subject and the tutor's experience at that level. The board tells you which questions to ask:

What should we agree before the first paid class?

  1. The unit of the fee. Is it per hour, per class or per month? A monthly fee needs a fixed number of classes behind it.
  2. The length of each class. An hour and a half at an hourly rate costs more than it first looks.
  3. Cancellations and make-ups. How much notice each side gives, and how a missed class is made up.
  4. The weather rule. When a class moves online, and whether the fee changes when it does.
  5. A review date. The end of a ten-week term or the next unit test, when you decide whether to extend, reduce or stop.

How should we plan a ten-week term of tuition?

Agree the weekly hours, the length of each class and the make-up rule before the term starts, and write them down. Two one-hour classes a week suits one middle-school subject. Review at the end of the term, using a unit test, and continue only if the work still needs it.

Is an Uttarakhand Board tutor cheaper than a CBSE tutor?

Not as a rule. The class, the subject and the tutor's experience at that level drive most quotes, and each tutor chooses their own rate. The board changes what you check: for UBSE, ask which state-board classes the tutor has taught recently and whether they teach in Hindi, English or both.

Will a tutor charge for a class we move online on a rain day?

That depends on what you agree, so settle it before the monsoon. One simple arrangement is the same rate for an online class held at the usual time, since the tutor still teaches the full lesson. Agree how much notice each side gives, and write the rule down with the rest of the fee terms.

No. The fee band is the same across the city. What changes on the edges is how many tutors live close enough for a regular slot, so the home shortlist may be shorter. Adding an online tutor for one specialist subject widens the choice without moving the band at all.

What does the band actually cover?

The range is wide because it covers very different kinds of teaching. A useful way to read it:

  • Toward the lower end: primary and middle-school classes, homework routines, reading and steady practice in a subject the child already follows.
  • In the middle: Classes 9 and 10 in maths and science, catch-up work after a weak unit test, and a tutor who teaches two related subjects.
  • Toward the upper end: Classes 11 and 12, separate tutors for physics, chemistry and maths, and JEE or NEET preparation.
  • Above it: specialists for JEE Advanced or IB HL, for students who need that depth.

The rate is only half of the cost. The other half is the number of hours, and that changes through the year far more than most families expect. The NXTutors pricing guide explains the factors class by class.

How do the hours change across a school year?

A plan that starts at two hours a week in the opening months may need four before the board exams. Planning for that change up front avoids a surprise in the final term. Two things follow. First, ask the tutor at the demo how they expect the hours to change, so the rise before the exams is agreed, not assumed. Second, if the upper total is out of reach, trim the opening months rather than the final ones; the revision phase is where a regular tutor helps most.

Does your side of the Gomti change the budget?

Location seldom sets a fee. It decides how many tutors can reach you easily, and so how many quotes you can compare. In Lucknow the Red Line does much of that work: it runs from Munshi Pulia in Indira Nagar, through the Trans-Gomti stations, Hazratganj and Charbagh, down Kanpur Road to Amausi and the airport. Homes near it draw tutors from along the whole line. Homes away from it depend on tutors who ride or drive.

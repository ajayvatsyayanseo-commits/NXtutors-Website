Does your part of Gangtok change what you pay?

It does not change the range. It changes how many tutors can reach you comfortably on a weekday evening. Gangtok is a long city that has grown along its main roads, and most tutors travel by shared taxi, on a two-wheeler or on foot for the last stretch, since the city has no railway or metro.

CBSE or ICSE/ISC: what should you tell the tutor?

Schools in Gangtok follow CBSE or CISCE (ICSE for Class 10 and ISC for Class 12), and CBSE schools in Sikkim come under the board's regional office in Guwahati. English and Nepali are the main languages of instruction in the city's schools, so mention the medium too if it is not English.

The board does not set the hourly rate. It decides which tutor fits. A tutor who has taught the ICSE English language and literature papers is a different match from one who knows the CBSE science book chapter by chapter. For current syllabus and exam details, families should check cbse.gov.in or cisce.org directly. Tutors who teach the CBSE syllabus are listed under CBSE home tutors in Gangtok.

Planning hours by stage, not by price

The number of hours you book shapes the monthly bill far more than a small gap between two hourly rates. Instead of starting from a budget figure, start from the stage your child is at and the questions that stage raises.

Whatever the stage, set a review point, such as the first unit test or the end of a term, when you and the tutor decide together whether to keep, cut or stop the hours.

How do the hill roads and the monsoon fit in?

Gangtok's homes climb the slopes on both sides of the roads, so a front door is often a flight of steps above or below the street. That affects timing, not price. Share directions from the nearest taxi point before the demo class, and agree a fixed weekday slot that leaves some margin around the evening traffic on the highway and the bypass.

During the monsoon, heavy rain can slow every journey. Rather than cancelling, many families agree at the start which classes move online on very wet days. Settle three things in advance: who decides that a class moves online, how much notice each side gives, and whether an online class carries the same rate as a home visit. Signs a quote is clear, and signs it needs questions

Two tutors can describe the same work in quite different ways, one per hour, another per month or per class. A clear quote makes comparison simple:

  • Clear: it names the unit (hour, class or month), the session length as clock times and the number of sessions each week.
  • Clear: it says whether online sessions and extra revision classes before board papers are charged at the usual rate.
  • Clear: it states whether practice work is set and checked between visits.
  • Needs questions: a monthly figure with no number of sessions attached.
  • Needs questions: a "class" with no stated length, so you cannot tell how much teaching time it covers.
  • Needs questions: no word on what happens to a class missed by either side.

What to confirm in writing

A short message that the tutor replies to is enough. Include how the fee is counted, the days and times, the address with the building name, floor and nearest taxi point, the notice period for cancellations, how missed classes are made up, which days move online in heavy rain, and the date of your first review. In a government housing colony, add that you will pass the tutor's name to the entrance in advance.

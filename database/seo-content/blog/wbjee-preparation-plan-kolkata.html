WBJEE is the state's common entrance test for undergraduate engineering, technology, pharmacy and architecture, and for most Kolkata students it is the last exam of a crowded Class 12 year. By the time it arrives, a Higher Secondary student has sat two Council semesters, an ISC or CBSE student has finished the board papers, and many have also written JEE Main. The preparation that works is the one that is planned around those dates from Class 11, not squeezed in after them.

This guide builds that plan. Every WBJEE detail below comes from the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board's website, wbjeeb.nic.in, and the WBJEE-2026 Information Bulletin linked from it; board calendars come from wbchse.wb.gov.in, cisce.org and cbse.gov.in. When this was written, the board's site was still carrying 2026 counselling notices and had published nothing about 2027, so treat any 2027 date as unknown until the new bulletin appears.

How the scoring should shape practice

Each subject mixes three categories of question. Category 1 carries one mark with a quarter-mark penalty; Category 2 carries two marks with a half-mark penalty; Category 3 can have more than one correct option, earns two marks for marking all of them, gives proportional credit for a partly correct answer with no wrong option, and scores zero, without a penalty, if any wrong option is marked. Ties in rank are broken first on fewer negative marks.

Three practice rules follow from that, and a tutor should drill them from the first mock:

  • Category 3: mark only certainties. One doubtful extra option wipes out the credit for the correct ones.
  • Categories 1 and 2: guess only after elimination. With two options left, the expected gain is positive; with four untouched options, it is roughly nil and adds to the negative-mark count that decides ties.
  • Keep a two-column error log. After every mock, sort each wrong answer into "did not know" and "should have left it". The second column holds the easiest marks a student can win back.

Splitting the week between subjects

With maths worth half the total, a common error is to give the three subjects equal time. A more useful starting split for the final months:

  • Mathematics, about half of WBJEE time. Seventy-five questions in two hours means speed in algebra, coordinate geometry and calculus decides the paper.
  • Physics, about a quarter. Numericals without a calculator, plus the modern-physics and semiconductor topics the bulletin lists.
  • Chemistry, about a quarter. Physical chemistry numericals, organic reactions, and the identification of acid and basic radicals, which a student who has only drilled MCQs can miss.

Adjust after the first three full mocks: the subject that loses most marks to the error log's second column gets the extra hour.

Checklist for the bulletin season

  • Download the current Information Bulletin from wbjeeb.nic.in as soon as it appears, and compare its syllabus appendix with your child's board syllabus line by line.
  • Note the registration window, and keep Class 10 and Class 12 documents and domicile details ready in advance.
  • Choose exam zones with the journey in mind; the 2026 bulletin let candidates pick three, several of them in and around Kolkata.
  • Print OMR-style sheets for every full mock and use a ballpoint pen; the bulletin says a marked response cannot be changed.
  • After the exam, watch for the model answer keys and OMR images, which the board posts for a short period with an online challenge facility.
  • For counselling, follow only the notices on the board's website; in 2026 they described an online decentralised counselling process.

Frequently asked questions

When is WBJEE 2027?

Not announced at the time of writing. The board describes the exam as usually held between April and May, preferably on a Sunday, and WBJEE-2026 was held on 24 May 2026. The 2027 date will appear in the new Information Bulletin and notices on wbjeeb.nic.in, so plan the final weeks only once those are out.

Can a Higher Secondary student prepare for WBJEE alongside the semesters?

Yes. The bulletin says the syllabus is closely aligned with the Class 11 and 12 curricula of recognised boards. The semesters already build multiple-choice speed; what WBJEE adds is negative marking and multi-correct questions, so a weekly WBJEE-style set from Class 12 onwards bridges the gap.

Do board marks matter if the rank comes from WBJEE?

Yes, for admission. The 2026 bulletin asked for a pass in English with at least 30% and, for engineering, at least 45% (40% for reserved categories) in three qualifying subjects, along with passes in theory and practical. The rank decides order; the board result decides eligibility.

Should my child skip doubtful Category 3 questions?

Mark only the options they are sure of. Category 3 gives proportional credit when some correct options are marked and no wrong one, but scores zero if any wrong option is marked. It carries no negative mark, so leaving a doubtful option out costs less than guessing it in.

Is WBJEE preparation better at home or online?

Both work. Full two-paper mocks with a tutor watching the clock suit home sessions, while short doubt-clearing during board months works well online. Where no maths specialist can reach you easily, an online maths tutor with a home tutor for physics and chemistry is a sensible split.

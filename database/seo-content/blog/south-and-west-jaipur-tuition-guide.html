South and west Jaipur is where the Pink Line begins and where much of the city's newer housing has grown: the planned colony of Mansarovar, the sectors of Chitrakoot and Pratap Nagar, the gated complexes of Jagatpura and the long corridors of Ajmer Road and Tonk Road. For a family looking for a home tutor, the two things that matter most here are whether the home is near a working station and which main road the tutor has to cross at the evening peak.

This guide covers three zones: Vaishali Nagar and West Jaipur; Mansarovar and Sanganer; and Malviya Nagar, Jagatpura and Tonk Road. Each zone has a table showing the nearest rail or metro link for every area, the usual homes and the visit routine, followed by advice on timing classes around the busiest roads.

What links the three zones?

The Pink Line starts at Mansarovar station, in the Padmavati Colony part of Nirman Nagar, and runs east along New Sanganer Road through New Aatish Market, Vivek Vihar and Shyam Nagar, then via Ram Nagar on Hawa Sadak towards Civil Lines. All these stations opened on 3 June 2015. Further south and east, tutors rely on the road network and on railway stations at Durgapura, Gandhinagar, Sanganer and Getor Jagatpura.

The Orange Line, a planned north-south route that is under construction, lists stops such as Gopalpura, Jaipur Airport, Sanganer PS, Haldighati Gate, Sitapura, Gandhinagar Station and Durgapura. None of them is open, so plan every trip on today's roads and trains.

Vaishali Nagar and West Jaipur

Vaishali Nagar is bounded by Queens Road to the east, the Delhi Bypass to the west, Ajmer Road to the south and Sirsi Road to the north. South of it, Ajmer Road, part of National Highway 48, runs out towards Ajmer, with Chitrakoot and Nirman Nagar along it and Shyam Nagar and Sodala closer to the Pink Line.

Mansarovar and Sanganer

Mansarovar was planned by the Jaipur Development Authority, and metro service reached it with the Pink Line in June 2015. To the south-east, Sanganer is a historic town known for textile printing and handmade paper and home to Jaipur International Airport, while Pratap Nagar, on Tonk Road, was developed largely by the Rajasthan Housing Board in numbered sectors.

Malviya Nagar, Jagatpura and Tonk Road

Tonk Road, part of National Highway 52, runs south from the city centre past Gandhinagar railway station at Tonk Phatak, Bajaj Nagar, Durgapura and Pratap Nagar towards Sanganer. Malviya Nagar and Jagatpura lie to its east, with the main road to the airport running close to Malviya Nagar.

How should we time a class around the main roads?

  • Ajmer Road and the bypasses: in Chitrakoot, Nirman Nagar, Sodala and along Ajmer Road, evening traffic is heavy, so either start soon after school or choose a tutor who can use the Pink Line.
  • New Sanganer Road and Shipra Path: in Shyam Nagar and Mansarovar, a tutor who comes by metro keeps better time in the evening than one on the road.
  • Gopalpura Bypass: late-evening or weekend slots avoid the busy daytime and early-evening stretch.
  • Tonk Road: ask for a tutor who lives on your side of the road, and allow spare time for evening classes in Durgapura, Pratap Nagar and Sanganer.
  • Malviya Nagar and Jagatpura: an earlier slot after school avoids the busiest market roads and the flyover approaches.

Frequently asked questions

Which Pink Line stations serve south and west Jaipur?

Mansarovar, New Aatish Market, Vivek Vihar, Shyam Nagar and Ram Nagar, all open since June 2015. They serve Mansarovar, Nirman Nagar, Shyam Nagar and Sodala directly, so a tutor can ride in and finish by auto or on foot. Vaishali Nagar, Malviya Nagar and Jagatpura have no metro station.

Can tutors use the Orange Line to reach Tonk Road or Sanganer?

No, not yet. The Orange Line is planned and under construction, with stops listed at places such as Gandhinagar Station, Durgapura, Gopalpura and Sanganer PS, but none is open. For now tutors come by scooter, car or auto, and railway stations at Durgapura and Sanganer help some trips.

Do gated complexes in Jagatpura and Vaishali Nagar need a gate pass for tutors?

Usually, yes. Gated complexes in both areas register visitors, so share the tutor's name, your tower and your flat number with security before the free demo. In independent houses and builder floors, common in Malviya Nagar, Sodala and Durgapura, the tutor comes straight to the door.

Which class slot works near Gopalpura Bypass?

Late evening or weekends. Gopalpura Bypass is lined with showrooms, shops and many coaching institutes, and the main road stays packed through the day into the early evening. A later weekday slot or a weekend morning gives a visiting tutor a steadier trip from Mansarovar, Durgapura or Nirman Nagar.

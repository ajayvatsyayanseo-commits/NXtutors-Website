Dehradun spreads across a valley from a compact centre around the Clock Tower, known locally as Ghantaghar. Four main roads carry the city outwards: Rajpur Road north towards the hills, Haridwar Road to the south-east, Chakrata Road to the west and Saharanpur Road to the south-west, with Sahastradhara Road forming a newer housing corridor to the north-east. Where you live along them decides what a tutor meets at the door and which slot keeps a weekly class steady.

This guide covers all five NXTutors zones in Dehradun and, for each locality, the homes, the route in and the timing that tends to hold.

How do tutors get around Dehradun?

By road. The city has no metro today: a Metro Neo network with an east-west and a north-south corridor, meeting at Ghantaghar, has been proposed and its detailed project report submitted, but it is not under construction. Tutors mostly ride their own two-wheeler, with autos and shared three-wheelers for the last stretch. Electric city buses have run since 2022, and the ISBT handles intercity buses.

Rajpur Road & Dalanwala

The northern zone climbs from the old centre towards Mussoorie: long settled lower down, greener and cooler higher up.

  • Rajpur Road: a wide, tree-lined main road with shops on the frontage and old bungalows, flats and builder floors behind. The lower stretch is busy at evening shopping time, so start early.
  • Karanpur: a compact, lively student neighbourhood of builder floors and small buildings above or behind shops. Give a precise shop or lane landmark, and come by two-wheeler rather than car.
  • Dalanwala: quiet, green lanes of spacious independent houses just east of the centre. Tutors walk up to the door and usually find parking outside.
  • Jakhan: larger family homes, villas and apartments on the upper stretch of Rajpur Road. Tutors from Kishanpur or Canal Road avoid the centre.
  • Rajpur: the old settlement at the head of the road, at the foot of the hills. Houses are often set back from the road, so send a map pin. Start earlier in winter, when the hillside gets dark and cold first.
  • Malsi: newer gated complexes and houses on the Mussoorie Road side, near the Malsi Deer Park. Register the tutor at the gate and ask about a regular pass after the demo.

Sahastradhara & Raipur

Much of Dehradun's newer housing lines the road to the Sahastradhara springs, with older colonies east towards Raipur.

  • Sahastradhara Road: a corridor of apartment complexes, builder floors and villas with most visits starting at a society gate. Avoid office closing time on the lower road.
  • Kishanpur: mostly apartments, between the Rajpur Road and Sahastradhara Road corridors, so it draws tutors from both. Check where a two-wheeler can be parked inside the complex.
  • Raipur Road: residential colonies of plots, houses and flats running east from the Sahastradhara crossing. Further out, fewer tutors live close by, so a tutor from Dalanwala or Karanpur, or an online specialist, is a practical choice.

Haridwar Road

Central, long-settled colonies, the Jogiwala junction and, down National Highway 7, the town of Doiwala near the Jolly Grant airport.

  • Race Course: plots and independent homes with wide roads close to the centre. Doorstep arrival with room to park; avoid office hours at the junctions towards Haridwar Road.
  • Nehru Colony: an established locality of houses, flats and markets near Fuwara Chowk. Market lanes are busiest in the evening, so share an exact lane landmark.
  • Jogiwala: an older junction area where Jogiwala Chowk links the highway and the ring road. Time classes outside the office rush at the chowk.
  • Doiwala: a town with its own nagar palika, mostly independent houses on plots. Its railway station sits on the Laksar line. Families often find a tutor in the town itself and use online classes for specialist subjects.

Vasant Vihar & Chakrata Road

The city's main western housing belt, from Vasant Vihar's colonies to newer townships at Kanwali and quieter streets at Prem Nagar.

  • Vasant Vihar: apartment complexes, builder floors and houses with good roads. Balliwala Chowk is the nearest main bus stop. Register the tutor at the gate.
  • Indira Nagar: also written Indra Nagar Colony, a fast-growing colony of plots and independent houses. Tutors arrive at the door and park on the street; tutors from neighbouring Vasant Vihar, Ballupur and GMS Road have a short trip.
  • Ballupur: built around Ballupur Chowk, a main junction on the way west, with flats, builder floors and houses. Share the building name and floor for flats, and avoid the evening peak at the chowk.
  • GMS Road: a shopping and office stretch with complexes on the road and quieter houses in the lanes behind. Shopping traffic builds in the evening, so start early.
  • Kanwali: apartments, houses and some larger gated townships between GMS Road and Patel Nagar. Ask the township gate about a regular visitor pass after the demo.
  • Prem Nagar: green, fairly quiet streets of plots and independent houses on the western edge, off Chakrata Road. A tutor living locally keeps evening classes steady. Homes inside defence areas have their own visitor rules.

Saharanpur Road & Clement Town

The south-western zone runs from the edge of the centre past the ISBT towards the highway to Saharanpur, ending in the green cantonment of Clement Town beside Rajaji National Park.

  • Patel Nagar: builder floors and independent houses next to an industrial area. Share the floor number, and avoid shift and office times.
  • Majra: a developing locality of three-bedroom apartments, houses and plots close to Saharanpur Road and the ISBT. Avoid the evening rush near the bus terminal.
  • Turner Road: mostly independent houses with some apartments near the ISBT. A new bypass is under construction nearby, so routes may shift for a while.
  • Clement Town: a cantonment run by its own board, with houses, villas and a strong Tibetan Buddhist presence. Check how visitors are admitted and give the tutor any entry details before the first class.

Seasons, boards and timing across Dehradun

In winter, evenings turn cold and dark early, so many families move classes earlier. In the monsoon, heavy showers can slow travel, and switching that day's class online keeps the week intact.

Children here study under the Uttarakhand Board of School Education, CBSE, or the CISCE's ICSE and ISC, with some families looking for IB or IGCSE help. Frequently asked questions

Is there a metro in Dehradun that tutors can use?

Not yet. A Metro Neo network has been proposed, with corridors meeting at Ghantaghar, but it is not under construction. Tutors travel by road, mostly on their own two-wheeler, with autos and shared three-wheelers for the last stretch. Choosing a tutor from your own zone keeps the trip simple and the start time steady.

Which part of Dehradun has the most gated apartment complexes?

The north-east, along Sahastradhara Road and in Kishanpur, along with Malsi and the newer townships around Kanwali. In these places most tutor visits begin at a society gate, so give the guard the tutor's name and flat number before the demo, and ask whether a regular visitor pass can be issued.

How should winter change our class timing?

Evenings turn cold and dark early in winter, most of all uphill in Jakhan, Rajpur and Malsi, so many families move the class earlier for those months. Agree the winter slot with the tutor in advance, and confirm whether its length and fee stay the same as the usual one.

We live in Doiwala. Can we still get a home tutor?

Yes, request one as usual. Doiwala is a town in its own right, and a tutor living there or along the highway is usually the most practical match. NXTutors looks for the nearest tutors first, then your zone and the rest of Dehradun, then online tutors for specialist subjects.

What should families in Clement Town or Prem Nagar check before a demo?

Whether the home sits inside a cantonment or defence area with its own visitor rules. If it does, find out how a visitor is admitted and pass any pass or entry details to the tutor before the first class. Civilian colonies and apartment buildings usually just register visitors at the gate.

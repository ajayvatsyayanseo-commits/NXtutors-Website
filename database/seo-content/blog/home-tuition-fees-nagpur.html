Does the board change the quote?

Nagpur families study under the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education, with the SSC at the end of Class 10 and the HSC at the end of Class 12, as well as CBSE, ICSE and ISC, and the international IB and IGCSE programmes. The board on its own seldom moves a rate much. Two things do:

  • How senior the class is. Up to Class 10, many tutors teach State Board, CBSE and ICSE students, so you have more quotes to compare. From Class 11, many families bring in one tutor per science subject.
  • How specialised the programme is. IB and IGCSE are assessed in their own way, and the pool of tutors who know them closely is smaller; quotes tend to sit high in the band, and an online specialist is often worth a look.

If your child is changing board, for example from the State Board to CBSE for Class 11, set aside extra hours in the first few weeks to close the gap.

Metro line or main road: does your part of Nagpur change the cost?

Location does not set the fee. What it changes is how many tutors can get to you without a long trip, which changes how many quotes you can compare. When is a higher quote the cheaper choice?

  • When a Class 12 student needs depth in one science subject and a generalist would need more hours to reach the same point.
  • When earlier tutors have not moved the results and board-specific experience is the missing piece.
  • When the goal is JEE or NEET, where specialist knowledge matters more than the hourly rate.
  • When an online specialist replaces a long cross-city trip that would limit how many classes fit into the week.

The lower part of the band usually suits junior classes and steady practice in a subject the child already follows, where regular attendance matters more than specialist depth.

A timeline from demo to first review

  1. Before the demo: list the class, board, subjects and weekly hours you can afford at both ends of the band. Compare the fees on your shortlist.
  2. At the free demo: watch how the tutor explains, checks understanding and sets practice. The checklist for demo classes sets out what to watch for.
  3. Before the first paid class: agree the slot, what happens after a cancellation, the rate for extra classes before exams, and whether payment is monthly in advance or after classes are held.
  4. After four weeks: review progress against the goal. If it is on track, keep or reduce the hours; if not, talk to the tutor before adding more.
  5. If the fit is wrong: ask for a different tutor; switching is free on NXTutors and comes with another free demo.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a home tutor cost in Nagpur?

Across NXTutors, most home-tuition sessions fall between ₹800 and ₹2,500 an hour; Classes 11–12, IB/IGCSE and JEE/NEET sit toward the upper end; specialists for IB HL or JEE Advanced can charge more. Each tutor sets their own fee, and you see it before the free demo class.

Is State Board tuition cheaper than CBSE tuition in Nagpur?

Usually not by itself. A Nagpur quote moves with the class, the subject and the tutor's experience. Up to Class 10, many tutors teach both boards, so you may see more quotes. HSC and CBSE classes in Classes 11 and 12 tend to be quoted higher.

How many hours a week does a Class 10 SSC student need?

Often about two hours a week for each subject that needs help, and more if the student is catching up. A student who needs maths and science might plan around four hours a week across one or two tutors. Discuss the plan at the free demo and review it after the first month.

Do tutors charge more to travel to Manewada or Koradi Road?

The band is the same citywide, and tutors set their own fees. What changes in areas without a metro, such as Manewada or Koradi Road, is how many tutors can get to you comfortably by road. NXTutors lists nearby tutors first and adds online tutors when the local choice is thin.

What should we agree with a Nagpur tutor before paying?

Four things: the weekly slot, what happens when either side cancels, the rate for extra classes before the SSC, HSC or board exams, and whether you pay monthly in advance or after classes are held. Agree a review point after four weeks, so the hours can be adjusted if progress stalls.

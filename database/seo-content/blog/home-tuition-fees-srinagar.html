How many hours a week does each stage usually need?

Hours are the part of the bill a family controls. Booking more than a child needs wastes money; booking too few leaves the tutor rushing. The patterns below are starting points, not rules.

Fewer, longer classes or more, shorter ones?

For a young child, attention runs out well before a long class ends, so shorter, more frequent visits usually work better. For a senior student on long problems, a longer visit saves setting-up time. Whatever you choose, remember that a longer class at an hourly rate costs more each time, and agree the length before the first paid class.

How does the long winter break change the plan?

Srinagar's school year has a long winter break, and December and January bring short, cold days. Families most often change tuition arrangements now, so decide in autumn rather than in the first cold week. There are four common choices.

JKBOSE, CBSE or ICSE: does the board change the quote?

Seldom on its own. Srinagar students sit JKBOSE papers (the Jammu and Kashmir Board of School Education, which serves schools across Jammu and Kashmir), CBSE papers, or the ICSE and ISC papers set by the CISCE; a few families need IB or IGCSE support. What really shapes a quote is the level being taught and how much of it the tutor has done before. The board is more useful for deciding what to ask:

  • JKBOSE: ask about JKBOSE classes taught in the last couple of years, and check that explanations come in the language your child answers papers in. For the session calendar, date sheets and notices, rely on the board's official website, and see JKBOSE tutors in Srinagar.
  • CBSE: ask how the tutor uses the board's sample papers and at what point in the year they start. See CBSE home tutors in Srinagar.
  • ICSE and ISC: check familiarity with the CISCE syllabus for your child's class and how essay-length answers are practised. See ICSE tutors in Srinagar.
  • IB and IGCSE: quotes usually land in the higher part of the range, and IB HL can go beyond it. An online tutor for Srinagar students is often the practical route.

What belongs in a simple written fee agreement?

A short message the tutor confirms is enough.

  1. The unit of charge. Hourly, by the class or monthly; a monthly figure should state how many classes it covers.
  2. Class length and days. Written as a time, not as "about an hour".
  3. Missed classes. The notice either of you owes the other, and when a lost class gets taught.
  4. The winter arrangement. Which of the four choices above you are taking, and from when.
  5. Online classes. When a class can move online and whether the rate changes when it does.
  6. Exam season. Whether extra revision classes before board exams are at the usual rate, and how many are planned.
  7. A review point. A unit test or term end when you decide together to continue, reduce or stop.

Should we keep paying a tutor through the winter break?

Only for classes that actually happen. Decide in autumn whether you will shift the slot earlier, move online, book a revision block or pause. If you pause, agree how much notice the tutor needs and whether your usual slot will be held for when school reopens.

Does a JKBOSE tutor charge less than a CBSE tutor?

There is no such rule. Quotes depend mainly on the class, the subject and how often the tutor has taught that level, and every tutor picks a rate independently. What the board alters is your checklist: for JKBOSE, ask about recent board classes taught and the language used to explain.

Is home tuition dearer in Buchpora or Nowgam than near Lal Chowk?

No. One range covers the whole of Srinagar. The difference lies in how many tutors live near enough for a fixed weekly class, so some neighbourhoods may see fewer home options. Pairing a home tutor with one online specialist gives more choice while the range itself stays put.

Is an online class priced the same as a home visit?

That depends on the tutor and on what you agree. Some tutors keep one rate for both because the teaching is the same; others set a separate online rate. Ask before the winter months, when online classes become more common, and write the answer down with the rest of your fee terms.

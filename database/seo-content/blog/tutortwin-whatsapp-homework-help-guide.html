What TutorTwin is

TutorTwin lives inside WhatsApp. There is no app to download and nothing new for your child to learn. The student sends a question, and TutorTwin sends back a step-by-step worked explanation, usually within seconds, at any hour.

A student can send:

  • a typed question;
  • a photo of homework, printed or handwritten;
  • a PDF, such as a worksheet;
  • a voice note.

It replies in English, Hindi or Hinglish, matching however the student writes. It also keeps a small memory for each student: the misconceptions they tend to repeat, what they are focusing on and their exam dates. That lets it make practice questions like one the student got wrong, set short quizzes, and send answers as a PDF for revision.

Subjects available at the time of writing are Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Computer Science, Social Science and Economics.

How a student uses it: two examples

Example 1: a photo of a maths question

Your child photographs a worksheet and types a short instruction: "solve Q3". Q3 reads: Solve 2x² − 5x + 3 = 0.

TutorTwin replies with the working, not just the answer, along these lines:

  1. Multiply the first and last coefficients: 2 × 3 = 6. Find two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to −5: these are −2 and −3.
  2. Split the middle term: 2x² − 2x − 3x + 3 = 0.
  3. Group and factorise: 2x(x − 1) − 3(x − 1) = 0, so (2x − 3)(x − 1) = 0.
  4. So x = 3/2 or x = 1.
  5. Check: putting x = 1 gives 2 − 5 + 3 = 0.

Two practical points. A photo needs a short instruction such as "solve Q3" or "explain step 2", otherwise TutorTwin does not know which question on the page your child means. And if the photo is blurry, TutorTwin will ask for a clearer one rather than guess.

Example 2: a Hinglish voice note

Your child records a voice note: "Photosynthesis mein light reaction aur dark reaction mein kya difference hai?" TutorTwin replies in Hinglish, explaining where each reaction happens, what goes in and what comes out. Your child can then ask a follow-up, such as "ek example do", in the same thread. If your child writes in English next time, it replies in English.

Good habits that make it work

Sit with your child for the first few sessions and agree on these habits.

  • Try first. Attempt the question for a fixed time before asking. Then send the attempt along with the question: "I got x = 2, where did I go wrong?"
  • Ask for hints. "Give me only the first step" or "give me a hint, not the answer" keeps your child doing the thinking.
  • Check answers. TutorTwin is an AI and can make mistakes. Compare important answers with the textbook, the answer key or the tutor, especially in maths and numerical science.
  • Use it to revise. Ask for five questions like the one your child got wrong, a short quiz before a test, or a PDF of the answers to keep with notes.
  • Tell it about exams. Mentioning an exam date and weak chapters lets TutorTwin's memory focus the practice.

What TutorTwin will not do

  • It will not pretend to be a person. It is an AI and never claims to be a person or a teacher.
  • It will not do assessed work as if it were your child's. TutorTwin is a tutor, not a ghost-writer. Projects, internal assessments and anything submitted for marks as your child's own work are off limits.
  • It is not always right. Answers, including maths answers, are not guaranteed.
  • It is not a search engine. It explains and works through problems. For the latest syllabus changes, exam dates or school notices, rely on the board website and the school.
  • Do not assume unlimited use. Each plan has its own terms, so check what your plan includes on the site.

Safety and privacy for under-18s

If your child is under 18, a parent or guardian should set up the subscription or give permission for it.

On AI-only plans, nobody reads the chats, except NXTutors staff where needed for support, safety or legal reasons. If you choose the HumanAI option, a named real teacher can read the chat and reply, and their replies are marked as theirs, so your child always knows whether a message came from the AI or from the teacher.

Sensible family rules help too: keep chats to schoolwork, do not share personal details such as a home address or school ID in the chat, and tell a parent if anything seems odd.

How TutorTwin fits alongside a human tutor

TutorTwin works best between classes. A human tutor sees how your child thinks, plans which chapters to fix first, spots patterns in mistakes and adjusts the pace. TutorTwin handles the doubt at 10 pm, the extra practice on a weak topic, and the quick quiz before a test.

A simple routine that works for many families:

  1. The tutor teaches a topic and sets homework.
  2. Your child attempts the homework and uses TutorTwin for hints when stuck.
  3. Your child notes the questions that needed help and takes them to the next class.
  4. The tutor goes over those questions and decides what to practise next.

If you want a real teacher involved in the chat itself, the HumanAI option lets a named teacher read the conversation and reply.

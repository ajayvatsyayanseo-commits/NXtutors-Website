East and south Hyderabad hold some of the city's most settled family neighbourhoods: apartment blocks around Uppal and Habsiguda, independent houses on the plotted colonies of Vanasthalipuram and Boduppal, and the dense older streets of Malakpet and Mehdipatnam. Much of it is well served by rail. The Blue Line ends at Nagole, the Red Line ends at LB Nagar, and between them a tutor from Ameerpet, Secunderabad or Kukatpally can reach many homes with one metro ride and a short auto. The south-west is different: Mehdipatnam, Tolichowki and Attapur depend on buses and roads, including the elevated expressway to the airport.

This guide covers three zones: Uppal, Habsiguda and Nacharam; Dilsukhnagar, LB Nagar and Vanasthalipuram; and Mehdipatnam, Tolichowki and Attapur. Each zone has a quick table of homes, arrival and timing for every locality, followed by the points that matter most for a tutor's visit.

Uppal, Habsiguda and Nacharam

The Blue Line's first stage, Nagole to Ameerpet, opened in November 2017, and was extended to HITEC City in March 2019 and to Raidurg in November 2019. From the eastern end, its stations here are Nagole, Uppal and Stadium, then Habsiguda, with Tarnaka next. Nagole, on the Inner Ring Road, is the eastern terminus and sits near the metro's Uppal depot.

Dilsukhnagar, LB Nagar and Vanasthalipuram

The Red Line opened from Miyapur to Ameerpet on 29 November 2017 and from Ameerpet to LB Nagar on 24 September 2018. Its stations in this zone are Malakpet, New Market, Musarambagh, Dilsukhnagar, Chaitanyapuri and LB Nagar, the southern terminus, with one more stop between Chaitanyapuri and LB Nagar. Kothapet lies along the highway to Vijayawada.

Mehdipatnam, Tolichowki and Attapur

The elevated expressway to the airport runs from Mehdipatnam to Aramghar, where it joins NH44, and has been open to traffic since 19 October 2009; it passes through Attapur. Mehdipatnam has a major city bus depot with routes to Secunderabad, Uppal, Gachibowli and beyond, and its nearest MMTS stations are Nampally and Lakdikapool. A six-lane flyover takes off at Tolichowki and runs through Shaikpet towards the IT district. There is no metro in this zone.

What should we sort out before the first visit?

  1. Nearest station or bus stop. Name it in your request, whether Nagole, LB Nagar, Chaitanyapuri or the Mehdipatnam depot, so the shortlist starts with tutors who can reach it.
  2. A landmark and a pin. A stadium gate, a market or an expressway pillar saves the tutor a phone call on the first visit.
  3. The gate routine. For apartment buildings, give security or the watchman the tutor's name and timing in advance; houses need nothing more than a bell.
  4. A slot after the peak. Across this side of the city, the Warangal highway, the LB Nagar crossroads and the Mehdipatnam junction are heaviest at office hours, so a class that begins once they ease is the most reliable.

Frequently asked questions

Which metro line serves Uppal and Nagole?

The Blue Line. Nagole is its eastern terminus, and Uppal and Stadium stations serve Uppal, so a tutor from Ameerpet, Begumpet or HITEC City can come on one line. From the station, a short auto ride covers the last stretch; in Uppal, drivers usually start from Uppal X Roads.

Is LB Nagar easy for tutors coming from other parts of the city?

Yes. LB Nagar is the southern end of the Red Line, which runs through Malakpet and the city centre to Ameerpet and Miyapur. A tutor from Kukatpally or Ameerpet can come by metro and walk or take an auto. Traffic at the crossroads is heavy at office hours, so start after the rush.

How do tutors reach Mehdipatnam and Attapur without a metro?

Mostly by bus or two-wheeler. Mehdipatnam's large bus depot links it to Secunderabad, Uppal and Gachibowli, and Attapur is reached through Mehdipatnam or by road from the Outer Ring Road side. In Attapur, share the expressway pillar number with the tutor along with the flat number.

Are home tutors available in Vanasthalipuram?

Tutors from nearby areas and online tutors are available on NXTutors. Most homes are independent houses on plotted colonies, so visits are simple doorstep arrivals. Tutors from the city usually ride the Red Line to LB Nagar and take an auto along the highway, so a fixed slot outside office hours suits both sides.

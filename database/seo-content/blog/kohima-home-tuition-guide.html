Kohima is built across hill slopes, and its neighbourhoods are municipal wards stacked above and below one another. Kohima Village, the old settlement that gave the city its name, holds the high ground to the north-east. The busy central stretch runs through Daklane, New Market and Midland, and the southern and western wards spread out towards Lerie, Agri Farm and Merhülietsa. For home tuition, the slope matters as much as the address: a tutor needs to know the ward, which level of the hill the house is on, and the nearest point a taxi can reach.

This guide walks through the four NXTutors zones in Kohima, ward by ward, with the practical details that help a first demo class start on time.

How do tutors get around Kohima?

By road. Buses and taxis are the city's main public transport, and Kohima is not on the rail network, so a tutor travels by taxi, bus or their own two-wheeler. Three things shape a visit more than anything else:

  • The upper or lower part of a ward. Several wards are split into named parts, such as Upper and Lower Naga Bazaar, Upper and Lower Bayavü Hill, and Upper, Middle and Lower Midland. Naming the part saves a search.
  • Above or below the road. Many homes sit on stepped paths off the road. Share a landmark, the nearest drop-off point and, if there is room, where a scooter can be left.
  • The central stretch at office hours. Tutors crossing between the northern and southern wards pass through the town centre, which is busiest when offices open and close. Slots that start after the evening peak hold better.

North Kohima & Kohima Village

The northern wards and the original village, joined to the centre through Naga Bazaar. See the North Kohima & Kohima Village zone page.

  • Kohima Village (Bara Basti): also called Kewhira, the settlement Kohima is named after, on the high ground to the north-east. Homes spread across the slope, so give a clear landmark and the closest point a taxi can reach.
  • Peraciezie: the first of the municipal wards, at the northern end of the city, with neighbourhood schools of its own. Tutors from the southern wards cross the centre to get here, so avoid the hour when offices close.
  • Bayavü Hill: split into Upper and Lower Bayavü Hill, with the head office of the Nagaland Board of School Education at the upper end. Lanes climb between the two levels, so tell the tutor which one the house is on.
  • Naga Bazaar: divided into Upper and Lower Naga Bazaar, linking the northern wards with Daklane and New Market. Say which part you are in when you book.
  • Kitsübozou: on the eastern side, right beside Kohima Village, incorporated in 1970. A tutor living in either neighbourhood can cover both with little travel.

Main Town & Midland

The middle of the city, where most cross-town routes meet. See the Main Town & Midland zone page.

  • Daklane: a central ward between Naga Bazaar and New Market, with Kitsübozou and Kohima Village a short climb to the east. One of the easier wards to reach from almost anywhere; after-school slots that begin once the peaks pass are smoothest.
  • New Market: one of the most familiar addresses in the city for taxis and buses, between Daklane and Midland. Parking on the central roads can be tight, so mention any space for a scooter.
  • Midland: made up of Upper, Middle and Lower Midland, just south of New Market. Say which part you live in; tutors from Officers' Hill and the Chandmari wards are close by.
  • Officers' Hill (Thegabakha): on the western side of the centre, between Midland, PR Hill and Merhülietsa. The hill roads can be narrow, so explain where to leave a two-wheeler and which steps lead to the house.

Chandmari & PR Hill

The southern side of central Kohima. See the Chandmari & PR Hill zone page.

  • Upper Chandmari: a ward of its own, with PR Hill to the west and Midland to the north. Its neighbourhood schools include a higher secondary school, so plan board-year tuition early.
  • Lower Chandmari: the separate neighbouring ward, with Midland to the north. Give a well-known landmark and the nearest taxi stop for a first visit.
  • PR Hill: covering PR Hill and Lower PR Hill on the south-western side, home to the Capital Cultural Centre, an easy name to give a taxi driver. Officers' Hill, Upper Chandmari and Agri Farm all border it.

Lerie & Agri Farm

The southern end and the western edge of the city, with the peak of Pulie Badze to the south-west. See the Lerie & Agri Farm zone page.

  • Lerie: at the southern end, grouped in one ward with New Ministers' Hill and New Reserve. Tutors from Agri Farm, PR Hill and the Chandmari wards are the most practical regular choice; those from the north may prefer weekend or later slots.
  • Agri Farm: a ward on the south-western side that also covers Upper Mediezie (Upper Agri), Electrical and Forest. Give the smaller neighbourhood name as well as Agri Farm.
  • Merhülietsa: on the western edge, including Lower Mediezie (Lower Agri), next to Agri Farm and Officers' Hill. Hillside paths can be steep, so share the nearest road point.

Boards, weather and timing across Kohima

Kohima students study under the Nagaland Board of School Education (NBSE), which conducts the HSLC examination for Class 10 and the HSSLC examination for Class 12, or under CBSE; families who need ICSE, IB or IGCSE support can add an online tutor. Mention the board and, for Classes 11–12, the stream, so each matched tutor knows the syllabus. For notices and dates, use the board's official site, nbsenl.edu.in. See Nagaland board tutors in Kohima and CBSE home tutors in Kohima.

Rain is heaviest from June to September. Allow extra time on wet evenings, and agree with the tutor at the start which classes move online on very wet days. Darker winter evenings are another reason to prefer an earlier slot for younger children.

Frequently asked questions

How do home tutors travel around Kohima?

By road, on their own two-wheeler or by taxi and bus, which are the city's main public transport. Kohima has no railway station, so every trip is by road. Tutors crossing between the north and the south pass through the busy centre, so slots after the evening peak are easier to keep.

What should we tell a tutor who has never visited our home?

Give the ward and its part, such as Upper Naga Bazaar or Lower Midland, a landmark, and the nearest point a taxi can drop off. Say whether the house sits above or below the road, and where a scooter can be parked. A shared map pin helps on the first visit.

Which wards are easiest for tutors to reach?

The central wards, Daklane, New Market and Midland, because most routes across town pass through or near them. In wards at the northern end or the southern and western edges, tutors from neighbouring wards are the natural match, and an online tutor can cover any subject that no nearby tutor teaches.

We live in Upper Chandmari. Why do directions matter so much?

Upper Chandmari and Lower Chandmari are separate wards with similar names, so a tutor can easily head to the wrong one. Say which ward you are in, add a landmark and the nearest taxi stop, and confirm the route with the tutor before the free demo class.

What happens to classes during the monsoon?

Rain is heaviest from June to September, and hill roads slow down on wet evenings. Many families agree that one class a week moves online in those months, or that any class can switch online on a very wet day. Settle this, and the rate for online classes, at the start.

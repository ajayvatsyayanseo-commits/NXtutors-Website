Ranchi grew outwards from a compact centre. Circular Road rings the old core, Kanke Road and Ratu Road fork north from it beyond Kutchery, the business districts of Lalpur and Doranda sit either side of the middle, and the heavy engineering township at Hatia and Dhurwa fills much of the south-west. Each part asks something different of a home tutor: a gate register in one place, a narrow lane in another, a market rush in a third.

This guide covers all four NXTutors zones in Ranchi: Kanke Road, Morabadi & Bariatu; Lalpur, Kokar & Namkum; Harmu, Argora & Ratu Road; and Doranda, Hinoo & Hatia. For each locality it notes the kind of homes a tutor visits, the route in, and when a weekly class is easiest to keep.

How does a tutor reach each zone?

Ranchi has no metro, so home tutors travel by road, usually on their own two-wheeler, by auto or e-rickshaw, or by cab. The railway helps tutors who live further out: Ranchi Junction is the headquarters of the Ranchi division of the South Eastern Railway, Hatia serves the south, Argora the west and Namkon the south-east. Two recent roads have also changed cross-town trips: the Kantatoli flyover, opened on 4 October 2024, and the Ratu Road elevated corridor, opened in July 2025.

Kanke Road, Morabadi & Bariatu

The northern zone is mostly residential. Kanke Road and Ratu Road are the two roads that fork off Circular Road beyond Kutchery, and Kanke Road carries on towards Kanke and its dam reservoir. Morabadi is known for the large Morabadi ground, and Bariatu looks towards the industrial areas of Kokar and Namkum to the south.

  • Kanke Road: older independent houses and newer apartment buildings, many with three-bedroom flats, in colonies such as Hatma behind the main road. Apartment buildings usually ask visitors to sign in at the gate; houses are a simple doorstep visit. Avoid the office rush on the road itself.
  • Morabadi: apartment buildings with two- and three-bedroom flats, with older houses in Van Vrindavan Colony, Adalhatu Colony and Dipatoli. When the ground hosts a large event, nearby roads fill up, so an online session may be easier that day.
  • Bariatu: Bariatu Road, Bariatu Housing Colony, Rani Bagan, Jora Talab and Sarhul Nagar, mostly ready apartment buildings with three-bedroom flats and houses in the housing colony. Roads get busy at peak hours, so after-school visits work better before the evening rush.

Lalpur, Kokar & Namkum

This zone runs from the commercial heart of the city out to its south-eastern edge. Lalpur, with Hindpiri, Upper Bazar, Lower Bazar and Doranda, is one of Ranchi's main business districts. Kokar and Namkum are among the city's established industrial areas, with residential pockets growing around them.

  • Lalpur: built around Lalpur Chowk, where Circular Road meets Old Hazaribagh Road, with homes in Burdwan Compound, Lower Burdwan Compound and Kantatoli. Ranchi Junction is close by and autos and e-rickshaws run much of the day, but parking near the main roads is limited, so tutors often take an auto for the last leg.
  • Kokar: a busy mixed locality on the Ranchi–Patna road and Hazaribagh Road, with mid-income apartment buildings and older houses in Tiril Basti, Bank Colony and Vasuki Nagar. The Kantatoli flyover carries traffic from the Kokar side over the Kantatoli junction. Apartment blocks usually need gate registration.
  • Namkum: quieter and greener than the centre, with plots, independent houses and newer gated enclaves in Barganwa, Tetry Toli and Amethiya Nagar. Namkon station on the Gomoh–Hatia line serves the area. Homes are spread out, so tutors usually ride their own two-wheeler.

Harmu, Argora & Ratu Road

The western zone holds several of Ranchi's established planned colonies. Harmu Housing Colony was set up in the early 1960s along Bypass Road, and Ashok Nagar was founded in 1975 as a cooperative housing society. Plotted layouts mean many homes here are independent houses on internal colony roads, with newer apartment buildings filling in over time.

  • Harmu: independent houses, newer high-rise apartment buildings, shops and government offices. Houses allow doorstep visits with street parking. Bypass Road is busy around office hours.
  • Argora: older houses and gated apartment societies around Argora Chowk, with its own railway station serving the surrounding colonies. Evening tutors do well to arrive a little early, as the chowk gets congested at peak hours.
  • Ashok Nagar: a planned plotted colony next to Harmu Housing Colony, Ashok Vihar and Ashok Kunj. Most homes are independent houses on internal roads, so a tutor can usually reach the door and park nearby. Argora and Ranchi Junction are the nearest stations.
  • Kadru: a compact locality that includes the A. G. Colony, with houses and small buildings that rarely have formal gate security. Parking in the narrow lanes can be tight, so a two-wheeler suits better than a car.
  • Pundag: a growing area next to Rishabh Nagar, Harmu and Chaputoli, with newer apartment enclaves and plotted streets. Pundag Road connects it with Harmu. Enclaves register visitors at the gate, so share the tutor's details in advance.
  • Ratu Road: a corridor running north-west, with shops on the main road and houses, plots and newer apartment buildings in the lanes behind, around Piska More and Vikas Nagar. The elevated corridor has eased the ground-level road, though it still gets busy around office hours.

Doranda, Hinoo & Hatia

The southern zone combines an old business district, the city's airport and a large public sector township. Ranchi's airport is in Hinoo. The heavy engineering plant set up in 1958 gave Hatia and Dhurwa their planned sectors and staff colonies, and the international cricket stadium sits within its premises. Further south, the Tupudana industrial area lies on the Ranchi–Khunti road.

  • Doranda: one of Ranchi's older districts, with homes in South Office Para, North Office Para and Shyamali Colony around its markets. Staff colonies and apartment buildings may ask visitors to register; houses in the office para areas allow doorstep visits. Market roads get busy in the evening.
  • Hinoo: mostly independent houses and small apartment buildings in colonies around the airport, with Doranda and Hindpiri close by. Roads near the airport and Doranda are busy at peak hours, so allow a little buffer for after-school sessions.
  • Dhurwa: the township laid out in sectors, with company quarters, independent houses, plots and newer affordable apartment buildings. Wide sector roads make parking easy. On cricket match days, roads near the stadium fill up, so families may switch to an online session.
  • Hatia: a quieter suburb with staff colonies, houses and low-rise buildings. Hatia station serves the south of the city and is a terminal for several trains, so tutors from elsewhere can come by train and finish by auto.
  • Tupudana: newer apartment enclaves with three-bedroom flats, plots and houses around Choybasatoli and River View Colony. The Ring Road passes close by. Homes are spread out, so tutors usually ride their own two-wheeler, and enclaves register visitors at the entrance.

Boards, classes and timing across Ranchi

Ranchi families study under the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), the state board, and under CBSE and CISCE (ICSE and ISC), with some looking for IB or IGCSE help. When you request a tutor, name the board and, for JAC, the medium your child writes the exam in, so the matched tutors teach in the same language. For Class 10 and Class 12, start regular lessons early in the session rather than in the final months.

Frequently asked questions

How do home tutors get around Ranchi without a metro?

By road, mostly on their own two-wheeler, by auto or e-rickshaw, or by cab. Tutors who live further out sometimes take a train to Ranchi Junction, Hatia, Argora or Namkon and finish by auto. Choosing a tutor from your own zone keeps the trip short and the start time steady.

Which part of Ranchi has the most plotted houses?

The western zone. Harmu Housing Colony, Ashok Nagar and Kadru are largely independent houses on colony roads, so tutor visits there are usually a doorstep arrival with parking nearby. Newer apartment buildings in these areas, and in Pundag, normally ask visitors to sign in at the gate.

When should classes be avoided near the Morabadi ground or the cricket stadium?

On days when the ground hosts a large event, or when a match is played at the stadium in the Dhurwa township, because nearby roads fill up. On those days an online session is often easier than a home visit, and the regular slot resumes afterwards.

Can we find a JAC tutor who teaches in Hindi?

Yes, ask for it in your request. Name the Jharkhand Academic Council as the board and say whether your child writes the exam in Hindi or English. NXTutors then matches two or three tutors who teach that board, and you can confirm the teaching language during the free demo.

An hourly rate is only one number in the bill. What a family actually pays depends on choices it controls: how long each class runs, how many classes a week, which subjects need a visit at all, and how many children share the tutor's evening. This guide works through those levers one at a time, prices a twelve-week term, looks at the boards Bhopal families follow, and explains how your part of the city shapes the shortlist rather than the fee.

Two children, one tutor: how should siblings be planned?

Many Bhopal homes have two children in school, and it is tempting to book one tutor for both. That can work well, but plan it honestly:

  • Count each child's hours separately. Two children learning different syllabuses need their own time, so a one-hour visit shared between them is really two half-hour classes.
  • Book back-to-back slots. One visit that covers both children, one after the other, saves the tutor a second journey and keeps your evening simpler.
  • Check the tutor covers both levels. A tutor strong in Class 10 maths may not be the right choice for a Class 4 child, and the reverse is also true.
  • Agree the arrangement in writing. Whether the tutor charges per child or per hour of the visit is the tutor's decision, so ask directly and note the answer.

Does your part of Bhopal change the cost?

An address in Bhopal seldom moves the hourly rate. What it moves is the number of tutors willing to travel to you, and so the number of quotes on the table. Two transport facts are worth knowing. The priority section of the metro's Orange Line, eight stations from the south of the city through Rani Kamlapati Station and MP Nagar up to Subhash Nagar, opened to passengers in December 2025; its northern stretch and the Blue Line are still to come. Separately, the old BRTS corridor on Hoshangabad Road was discontinued in 2023 and dismantled from 2024, so buses now run in mixed traffic.

  • Arera Colony, Shahpura and Kolar Road: settled sectors and a long corridor to the south. Tutors from Arera Colony and Shahpura often cover Kolar Road, which has no rail or metro station.
  • MP Nagar, TT Nagar and Shivaji Nagar: the central belt, with MP Nagar station on the open section, so tutors from across the city can reach it.
  • Hoshangabad Road, Misrod and Katara Hills: gated townships on the south-eastern corridor, where tutors from the same stretch are the easiest to schedule.
  • BHEL, Awadhpuri and Ayodhya Bypass: the township sectors and the eastern colonies, reached by road, where shift timings shape traffic.
  • Old City, Lalghati and Bairagarh: dense lanes north of the Upper Lake and the western suburbs, where tutors often park and walk the last stretch.

On the newer edges, such as Katara Hills, Misrod or the far end of Kolar Road, fewer tutors live close by. The band stays the same there, yet a fixed weekly slot matters more, and moving a second subject online can help; our comparison of home and online tutors weighs the two. Frequently asked questions

What might one school term of tuition cost?

Take the weekly hours, multiply by twelve and price the result at ₹800 and at ₹2,500. A plan of two one-hour classes each week gives 24 hours a term, which is ₹19,200 at ₹800 an hour and ₹60,000 at ₹2,500 an hour. Add a little for extra classes before exams.

Is a shorter class cheaper for a young child?

Each visit costs less, because the rate is per hour: a class of three-quarters of an hour is ₹600 at ₹800 an hour. Three such classes a week can still add up to more than two one-hour classes, so compare the weekly hours, not just the price of one visit.

Is an MP Board tutor cheaper than a CBSE or ICSE tutor?

Not as a rule. Tutors set their own rates, and the class, the subject and their experience count for more than the board. The board matters when choosing: look for a tutor who teaches MP Board students every year and knows how the state board's papers are set.

Is tuition cheaper near an Orange Line station?

The rate stays with the tutor, but the choice can grow. A home close to MP Nagar or Rani Kamlapati Station on the open section is within reach of tutors who travel by metro, which can mean more quotes to compare. The northern stretch of the line has not opened.

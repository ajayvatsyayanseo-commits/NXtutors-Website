Which subjects actually need a tutor?

Before you ask anyone for a quote, sort your child's subjects into three groups. The sorting decides the budget more than any single hourly rate does.

  • Needs a tutor at home: the subject where the child is furthest behind, or where written working has to be watched line by line, such as maths from Class 6 onwards or numericals in physics.
  • Can go online: a subject the child already follows but wants to sharpen, or a specialist syllabus such as IB or IGCSE where the right tutor may not live near you.
  • Needs no tutor yet: subjects where marks are steady. Leave them out and review after the next unit test.

Which boards do Pune families plan for?

The board decides the syllabus a tutor has to know well, and that is one of the main things behind a quote.

  • Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education: the state board, with the SSC at the end of Class 10 and the HSC at the end of Class 12. Ask for a tutor who teaches that board regularly, not only CBSE.
  • CBSE: the national board, with the same syllabus from city to city, which helps families who may move. Junior-class help usually sits lower in the band; Class 10 and the senior years sit higher.
  • ICSE and ISC: the CISCE boards. Look for a tutor who knows the CISCE syllabus for your class and marks written work closely.
  • IB and IGCSE: these programmes have their own assessment style, and fees sit toward the upper end. A specialist online tutor is often the practical route when none lives near you.

JEE and NEET preparation alongside Class 11–12 also sits toward the upper end. Budget for the entrance work and the board work together, not as two separate plans that compete for the same evenings.

Location rarely sets a fee on its own. It decides how many tutors can reach you on a weekday evening, and so how many quotes you can compare. What should we settle before the first paid month?

  1. The fee and the format. Hourly rate, class length and whether any classes are online.
  2. Cancellations. What happens when either side cancels, and how much notice is needed.
  3. Exam season. Whether extra hours before the SSC, HSC, board or school exams are at the same rate.
  4. Payment. Monthly in advance or after the classes are held.
  5. Progress. How the tutor will report it, and when you will review the plan together.

Is a Maharashtra State Board tutor cheaper than a CBSE tutor?

Not as a rule. The class and subject matter more than the board name, and each tutor sets their own fee. What matters is that the tutor teaches your board regularly, since the SSC and HSC syllabus differs from CBSE's. Ask about this at the free demo.

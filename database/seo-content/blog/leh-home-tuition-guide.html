Leh sits on the bank of the Indus, with a compact town around the Main Bazaar and a string of villages spread along the river valley on either side. For home tuition, that shape matters more than any map grid. A home in the old town is reached on foot through narrow lanes, a home in Choglamsar sits beside the highway, and a home in Stok or Thiksey is a house among fields where a tutor arrives by car or two-wheeler.

This guide covers the three NXTutors zones around Leh, locality by locality, with the practical details that help a tutor find your door and keep the same slot through the year.

How do tutors get around Leh?

By road. There is no metro or railway, so tutors travel by two-wheeler or car, and walk the last stretch where lanes are narrow. Two road facts shape most routes:

  • Choglamsar is the junction. At Choglamsar the Leh–Manali Highway leaves the Indus valley and turns north towards the town, and two circular roads run between Choglamsar and Leh, one through Spituk and the other through Saboo.
  • A bridge links both banks. Since 2019 a suspension bridge over the Indus has joined Choglamsar with Stok and Chuchot Yokma, so a tutor based in Choglamsar can cross the river directly.

Leh Town Centre

The commercial heart of Leh and the residential localities closest to it. See the Leh Town Centre zone page.

  • Main Bazaar & Old Town: family homes sit alongside shops and government offices, and the old town climbs the slope behind the bazaar. Old-town addresses are hard for a newcomer to find, so share a clear lane landmark; the tutor will usually walk the final stretch.
  • Housing Colony: a residential colony with its own main market and a community hall, which make easy meeting points for a first visit. A tutor already teaching around the bazaar can often add a home here on the same evening.
  • Changspa: a hillside locality on the edge of town, with homes, lanes and fields; some addresses also name Sheldan. Give the house name and nearest lane marker, since the uphill lanes suit a two-wheeler more than walking.
  • Sankar: a quiet residential village-locality just north-west of the town, among trees. House numbers are not always used, so name the monastery lane or a nearby shop and say where a vehicle can stop.

Choglamsar, Spituk & West

The junction at Choglamsar, the two circular roads into town and the long valley to the west. See the Choglamsar, Spituk & West zone page.

  • Choglamsar: a census town on the Indus where homes share space with government offices, nurseries and sports grounds. Tutors can reach it from either circular road; in the summer months, pick a slot away from the busiest highway hours.
  • Saboo: a residential village of family homes and fields that shares its postal area with Choglamsar; addresses include Saboothang. Homes are spread out, so give the cluster name, and agree parking if the tutor comes by car.
  • Spituk: a census town south-west of the town, mostly family homes in the valley, on the second circular road. A landmark on the main road helps on the first visit, and an earlier slot avoids the evening build-up towards town.
  • Phyang: one of the largest villages in the area, spread along a valley west of Leh in eight clusters, from Phulungs and Tsakma to Gaon and Mankhang. Always tell the tutor your cluster; weekend or late-afternoon slots suit tutors driving out from town.

Indus Valley South & East

Villages on the far bank of the Indus and up the valley to the east, where houses stand among fields. See the Indus Valley South & East zone page.

  • Stok: a village on the southern bank, with traditional and newer family houses. The bridge from Choglamsar brings tutors across the river directly; give a clear landmark, because houses are spread out.
  • Chuchot: three villages, Chuchot Gongma, Chuchot Yokma and Chuchot Shamma, with farmland near the river. Say which of the three you live in, since each has its own lanes, and keep space for a vehicle near the house.
  • Shey: family houses among fields along the Indus, on the road east of the town. A tutor teaching in Thiksey or Choglamsar can often add Shey to the same trip, which makes a weekly slot easier to fix.
  • Thiksey: a village that is the headquarters of its own block and tehsil, with homes spread out among fields. Look for a tutor who already teaches in Shey or Choglamsar, and share the cluster name for the first visit.

Board, school year and the cold months

Most students around Leh follow CBSE. CBSE's affiliation list carries Ladakh as its own entry, with government high and higher secondary schools in Leh town and in places such as Housing Colony, Stok, Phyang, Thiksey and Chuchot among the affiliated schools. Put the board, class and, for Classes 11–12, the stream in your request, and check cbse.gov.in for current syllabus and exam details. Tutors for the CBSE syllabus are listed under CBSE home tutors in Leh.

Winters are long and cold, roughly from late November to early March, and schools take a long winter break. Treat this as a timing question. Many families move home visits to midday, when the day is warmest, or switch some classes to an online tutor for Leh students. On very cold days, allow extra time for travel and agree in advance which classes move online.

Frequently asked questions

How do home tutors usually reach homes in Leh?

By two-wheeler or car, since the area has no metro or railway. In the old town behind the Main Bazaar they usually walk the final stretch through narrow lanes. Share a lane landmark, the house name and the place where a vehicle can stop, so the tutor can plan the route before the first visit.

We live in a village along the Indus. Can we still get a home tutor?

Often, yes, especially if a tutor already teaches nearby. Tutors in Choglamsar can cross the bridge to Stok and Chuchot, and a tutor teaching in Shey can often add Thiksey. Where no nearby tutor fits, NXTutors looks across the zone and the town, and online classes cover the rest.

What should we tell a tutor about a home in Phyang?

Name your cluster. Phyang is spread along a valley in eight clusters, including Phulungs, Tsakma, Changmachan, Gaon and Mankhang, so the village name alone is not enough. Add a landmark and a phone number, and suggest a weekend or late-afternoon slot that suits a tutor driving out from town.

How do families handle tuition in the long winter break?

Most decide before winter arrives. Common choices are a midday home slot when the day is warmest, moving some or all classes online, or a revision block before board papers. Agree when the change starts, whether class length stays the same, and how much notice each side gives for a cancelled visit.

Which board do Leh tutors usually teach?

Mostly CBSE, because government high and higher secondary schools across Leh district appear on CBSE's affiliation list alongside private schools. Name the board and class in your request. If your child follows a different syllabus and no nearby tutor teaches it, an online tutor is the practical route for that subject.

Three administrations share the tricity: Chandigarh is a union territory, Mohali is in Punjab and Panchkula is in Haryana. So besides CBSE, ICSE/ISC and IB/IGCSE, many families follow the Punjab School Education Board (PSEB) or the Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH). This guide starts with the board, builds the cost one subject at a time, looks at your part of the tricity, and ends with the points in the year to recheck the budget.

Does the board change what a tutor charges?

The board rarely sets a fee by itself. What it does decide is how many tutors can teach your child well, and a narrow pool usually means fewer quotes to compare. The table sets out what to check for each board and where quotes tend to sit.

Does your part of the tricity change the cost?

Location rarely moves the fee itself. What it shapes is the size of your shortlist and which weekday slot holds. The tricity has no metro running yet: the revived Chandigarh Metro, cleared in July 2024, is planned to be built in its first phase between 2027 and 2034. Until then tutors travel by car, scooter, city bus or auto, and the Chandigarh Transport Undertaking runs buses from inter-state bus terminals in Sectors 17 and 43.

When should a tricity family check the budget again?

  1. Before the demo. Fill in your subject rows with the real fees on your shortlist.
  2. After the first four paid weeks. A subject that is ahead of plan can lose a weekly class; one that is behind needs a conversation with the tutor before any extra hours.
  3. Ahead of school exams and pre-boards. Agree in advance whether extra classes are charged at the same rate, so the busiest weeks hold no surprises.
  4. In festival and match weeks. The Navratra crowds at the Mata Mansa Devi temple and match days at the Sector 16 stadium and the Mohali stadiums can hold tutors up; agree to move those classes online or to another day.
  5. At the start of the new session. A new class means new subject rows, so re-price the plan first.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a home tutor cost in Chandigarh?

Across NXTutors, most home-tuition sessions fall between ₹800 and ₹2,500 an hour; Classes 11–12, IB/IGCSE and JEE/NEET sit toward the upper end; specialists for IB HL or JEE Advanced can charge more. Each tutor sets their own fee, and you see it before the free demo class.

Is home tuition cheaper in Mohali or Panchkula than in Chandigarh?

No, one band covers Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and Zirakpur alike. A quote mostly reflects the class, the board, the subject and the tutor's experience, and every tutor names their own figure. Your address changes how many tutors can travel to you, and so how many quotes you get to weigh.

Do PSEB or BSEH students pay less than CBSE students?

Not as a rule. On any board, junior classes tend to sit lower in the band and Classes 11–12 toward the upper end. What matters more is finding a tutor who has taught that board recently, in the right medium, so ask about it at the free demo before comparing fees.

How do we work out a monthly total for two subjects?

Price each subject on its own, then add them. Three one-hour maths classes and two one-hour science classes a week make 20 hours in four weeks: ₹16,000 at the lower end of the band and ₹50,000 at the upper end. Your shortlist's actual fees then fix the real figure.

Does the lack of a metro make home tuition harder to arrange?

It mainly affects timing. Tutors in the tricity travel by car, scooter, city bus or auto, so a class that starts before the evening office traffic on Madhya Marg, Dakshin Marg or the Zirakpur highways holds up well. Families in newer areas often mix a home tutor with online classes.

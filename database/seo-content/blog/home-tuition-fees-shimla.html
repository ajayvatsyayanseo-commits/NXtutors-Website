How many hours should we book each week?

Tutors quote by the hour, so weekly hours matter as much as the rate. Start with the fewest hours that cover the need. These are planning starting points, not a prescription:

  • Classes 1–5: one or two short sessions a week, mainly reading, number work and homework habits.
  • Classes 6–8: two sessions in one subject, usually maths or science, to close gaps before they widen.
  • Classes 9–10: two longer sessions per subject, with past-paper practice from the second term.
  • Classes 11–12: two or three longer sessions per subject, since the concepts are new and the written work is longer.
  • Class 12 with JEE or NEET: a split plan, with some hours for the board syllabus and some for entrance practice, agreed in writing.

Review the hours at each unit test: trim a session when things steady, or extend one subject for a few weeks when a chapter keeps slipping.

How do winter and the monsoon affect what we pay?

Weather rarely changes a tutor's hourly rate; it changes how many classes actually happen. In Shimla, heavy rain in the monsoon, fog on the western side and snow in winter can all slow travel, so agree in advance what happens on those days.

HPBOSE, CBSE, ICSE or IB: does the board change the fee?

Rarely on its own. Families in Shimla follow the Himachal Pradesh Board of School Education (HPBOSE), which holds the Class 10 (Matric) and Class 12 (Plus Two) examinations and has its headquarters in Dharamshala, or CBSE, or the CISCE's ICSE and ISC, and a smaller number need IB or IGCSE help. The class and subject decide most of the quote; the board decides what you check at the demo.

  • HPBOSE: has the tutor taught state-board classes recently, and can they explain in Hindi, English or both to match the language of your child's answers? For exam notices, use the board's own website. See Himachal Board tutors in Shimla.
  • CBSE: which CBSE classes have they taught lately, and when do they start the board's sample papers? See CBSE home tutors in Shimla.
  • ICSE and ISC: do they know the council's syllabus for this class, and how do they train longer written answers?
  • IB and IGCSE: expect quotes toward the upper end of the band, and more for IB HL; an online tutor for Shimla students is often the realistic route.

When does online tuition make sense for a Shimla family?

Online tutors also charge by the hour, and there is no journey at all. They suit a specialist subject whose right teacher lives across the city or in another state, and they work as a back-up on weather days. Many families keep a home tutor for the main subject and add one subject online.

What should we settle before the first paid class?

  1. The billing unit. Hourly, per class or monthly; a monthly figure should say how many classes it buys.
  2. Class length. An hour and a half at an hourly rate adds up faster than it first seems.
  3. Missed classes. Who gives notice, how early, and when the make-up happens.
  4. Rain, fog and snow. The trigger for an online class and its terms.
  5. A review point. Usually the next unit test, to keep, cut or end the hours.

That is for you and the tutor to agree, ideally before winter starts. A common arrangement is the usual rate for an online class held at the normal time and length, because the tutor still teaches the full lesson. Write the rule down together with the notice period and the make-up terms.

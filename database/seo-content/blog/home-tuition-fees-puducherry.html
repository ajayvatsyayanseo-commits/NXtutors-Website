Has the move to CBSE changed what families pay for?

Puducherry has no school board of its own. In April and May 2024 the Directorate of School Education ran orientation programmes for heads of institutions, inspecting officers and teachers under the title "Ensuring a smooth swap from State Syllabus to CBSE Syllabus". The Directorate's result-analysis pages list Class 10 results as SSLC up to 2024 and as CBSE 10 from 2025, and Class 12 results as +2 up to 2024 and as CBSE 12 from 2025. From 2026 it also publishes separate State Board (SSLC and +2) result analyses for private schools. You can read these pages on the Directorate of School Education website.

The switch does not set a new price for tuition; it changes the kind of tutor a child needs. A student who began on the state syllabus and is finishing under CBSE may have gaps where the two courses differ, and that belongs in the conversation before you agree a fee.

What should one paid hour contain?

An hour is worth what happens inside it. A well-run class checks last week's work, teaches one idea with the child doing the working, practises up to the standard of a school test, and ends with a short task for the week. A tutor who spends most of the hour finishing school homework gives less for the same rate, so ask how each tutor divides a class and watch for it at the demo. Which fee questions matter at each stage?

Classes 1 to 8

Younger children tire quickly, so shorter, more frequent classes usually beat one long sitting. In the middle years, ask whether one tutor can cover maths and science, and when a subject can be dropped once marks hold steady across two unit tests.

Classes 9 and 10

This is where the syllabus change matters most for many Puducherry families. Ask how the tutor will connect earlier state-syllabus chapters with the CBSE course.

Classes 11 and 12

Physics, chemistry, maths, biology and accountancy usually need separate attention. Ask whether one tutor can handle two of them or two specialists would serve better. See physics, chemistry, biology and accountancy tutors in Puducherry.

Does your part of Puducherry change the fee?

No. One range covers the whole town. What differs is how many tutors live close enough to keep a fixed weekly slot.

Heritage Town

In White Town, the Tamil Quarter and Muthialpet, most visits are to houses with their own street door. The seafront and shopping streets are crowded in the evenings and at weekends, so weekday slots are easier to keep.

Mudaliarpet & Ariyankuppam

The Cuddalore road links Mudaliarpet with Ariyankuppam and the villages beyond. Further south, in Veerampattinam or Thavalakuppam, the practical pool is tutors living in Manavely, Ariyankuppam and the nearby villages, so expect a shorter home shortlist rather than a higher fee.

Lawspet & ECR

Lawspet, one of the most densely populated parts of Puducherry, and the settled colonies of Kamaraj Nagar are reachable from much of the town. Reddiarpalayam & Villianur

Saram sits close to the main bus stand, and Reddiarpalayam is a largely residential locality that most tutors reach by two-wheeler or bus. How do festivals and exam season affect the number of classes?

The rate usually stays put through the year; the number of classes that actually happen does not. Plan for two kinds of disruption.

The first is local festival days. The Aadi-month car festival at Veerampattinam brings crowds to the Ariyankuppam side, and its fifth Friday is a public holiday declared by the Government of Puducherry; the Masi Magam gatherings fill the beaches at Veerampattinam and Vaithikuppam. Agree in advance whether such a day means a moved class, an online class or a skipped one.

The second is exam season. Before Class 10 and 12 board exams, many families add revision classes. Agree how many, at what rate, and when the extra classes stop, so a short revision block does not quietly become a permanent increase.

Home or online: which subjects need a visit?

A young child usually does better with a tutor in the room; a senior student working through physics numericals or accountancy problems can often learn as well on screen, with tutors from across India to choose from. Many families keep a home tutor for one or two subjects and add an online tutor for Puducherry students for the specialist one. Ask each tutor whether an online class costs the same as a home visit. What makes a fee quote easy to trust?

  • Clear: the unit is stated (per hour, per class or per month, with the number of classes), the class length is written down, notice rules apply both ways, and there is a review point such as the next unit test.
  • Worth more questions: the fee stays vague until after several classes, a long block is to be paid in advance before any teaching, or extra revision classes come with no stated number or end date.

Does a tutor for the state-board syllabus charge less than a CBSE tutor?

There is no such rule. A quote mostly follows the class, the subject and how often the tutor has taught that level. The syllabus changes what you check instead: ask which state-board or CBSE classes the tutor has taught recently, and how they would fill gaps for a child who has moved between them.

My child moved to CBSE after starting on the state syllabus. What should we ask a tutor?

Ask how they will find the chapters your child missed or learned differently, and how long that catch-up should take. A good tutor tests early, makes a short list of gaps and works through them alongside current school topics, rather than reteaching everything from the start or ignoring the older course.

Is home tuition dearer in Kalapet or Villianur than in the old town?

No. The same range covers all of Puducherry. What changes on the edges of town is how many tutors live close enough for a fixed weekly class, so the home shortlist may be shorter. Adding one online tutor for a specialist subject widens the choice without moving the range.

Should we pay for a class cancelled on a festival day?

That depends on what you agree at the start. A simple rule is that a class cancelled with fair notice by either side is moved, not charged, and that a class held online at the usual time costs the same as a home visit. Write the rule down with the rest of your fee terms.

Vijayawada has several distinct parts. The old city, One Town, lies west of the centre near the Kanaka Durga temple hill; the commercial core surrounds the railway junction at Gandhinagar and Governorpet; eastern localities line Bandar Road and Eluru Road; and Gollapudi continues west along the road towards Hyderabad.

This guide covers all five NXTutors zones in the city, noting for every locality the homes, the route in and the easiest time for a weekly class.

How do tutors get around Vijayawada?

A metro network is planned but not yet running, so home tutors travel by two-wheeler, auto, city bus or car, and the railway helps those who live further out. Vijayawada Junction serves the centre, Gunadala, Madhura Nagar and Ramavarappadu the east and north, and Rayanapadu, a satellite station, the west. Three road links shape cross-town trips: the Inner Ring Road from the Gollapudi Y-junction to the Ramavarappadu ring, opened in July 2016; the Kanaka Durga flyover from Bhavanipuram, opened in 2020; and the Benz Circle flyover, whose second phase opened in November 2021.

Central Vijayawada

The commercial heart of the city, largely in the Vijayawada Central mandal formed in 2018. Homes sit behind busy shop frontages, so parking and finding the right lane are the practical questions.

  • Governorpet: a central business area and textile trading street with a state transport bus station. Families mostly live in the lanes behind; send a lane landmark and fix an evening slot once the markets quieten.
  • Gandhinagar: beside the railway junction, where trains, city buses and autos meet, so tutors arrive easily; avoid train-time crowds when fixing the class.
  • Suryaraopet: a mix of apartment buildings, independent houses and open plots, bordered by Labbipet, Governorpet and Moghalrajpuram. Check whether the home is a flat with a building gate or a house with doorstep arrival.
  • Labbipet: mostly two- and three-bedroom flats near Bandar Road, one of the city's main shopping streets. Share the building and flat number with the guard before the demo.
  • Moghalrajpuram: apartments with some builder floors and plots beside low hills known for rock-cut cave shrines from around the fifth century. For houses near the hills, a cave or temple landmark helps a tutor find the door.

Benz Circle & Patamata

This zone lies around Benz Circle, the busiest junction in Vijayawada, where National Highways 16 and 65 meet and Bandar Road begins. Patamata was merged into the municipal corporation in 1985, and Ramavarappadu joined the metropolitan area by a government order in 2017.

  • Benz Circle: low-rise and mid-rise flats with some plots around the junction. Buildings usually ask visitors to sign in; a tutor from your side of the junction avoids crossing it at office hours.
  • Patamata: two- and three-bedroom apartments and residential plots between Benz Circle and Auto Nagar. Register the tutor with the guard and share the block and flat number before the first class.
  • Currency Nagar: builder floors, independent houses and apartments between the centre and the Kanuru side. Colony houses usually mean doorstep arrival; set a class time outside the evening peak on the roads towards Benz Circle.
  • Ramavarappadu: a residential hub at the eastern end of the Inner Ring Road, with its own station on the loop line. The ring road lets a tutor from the west come across without going through the centre.

Eluru Road & North

A largely residential zone with many independent houses on colony streets. Eluru Road, one of the city's two main arterial roads, runs into Gunadala.

  • Gunadala: traditional houses and newer apartment buildings around the hill with its Marian shrine. The shrine's annual festival in February draws very large crowds, so move classes online or reschedule for those days.
  • Machavaram: independent houses, villas and plots surrounded by a ring of colonies. Colony lanes look alike on a first visit, so share a landmark.
  • Satyanarayanapuram: mainly two- and three-bedroom flats with some villas and older houses, between the central business streets and the northern colonies. Give the guard the tutor's name before the first class.
  • Ajit Singh Nagar: a settled neighbourhood of independent houses and plots with some two-bedroom flats. Lanes can be narrow, so a two-wheeler suits better than a car, and Madhura Nagar station is close for local trains.

One Town & West

From the old city out along the highway towards Hyderabad. One Town takes in neighbourhoods such as Arjuna Veedhi, Islampet, Kothapet and Winchipet; Bhavanipuram grew as commercial activity moved west in the 1990s.

  • One Town: older houses along the lanes of the old city, around some of its busiest markets. A two-wheeler or e-rickshaw is easier than a car, and a precise lane landmark helps.
  • Vidyadharapuram: apartments, villas and plots close to the Kanaka Durga temple on the hill above the Krishna. Roads near the temple become very crowded during Navaratri, so plan online sessions or shifted timings for that season.
  • Bhavanipuram: shops and homes bordering NH 65 and the bypass road. The flyover has eased trips to the centre, but highway traffic is heavy at office hours, so a tutor from this side is simplest.
  • Gollapudi: a suburb on the Hyderabad road beside the river, with apartments, independent houses and plots. State transport buses link it with the bus station and the railway station, and Rayanapadu station serves this side of the city.

Kanuru & Poranki

The eastern edge along Bandar Road. Kanuru, Tadigadapa, Poranki and Yenamalakuduru were merged into a single municipality at the end of 2020, and Penamaluru heads its own mandal beside the Krishna.

  • Kanuru: mostly apartments, then plots, builder floors and houses, in an area known as one of the city's educational hubs. Roads near the main teaching institutions are busiest when their classes start and finish, so set the tuition slot away from those hours.
  • Tadigadapa: villas and larger three-bedroom flats in recent projects, with no railway station of its own. Register the tutor at the gate and ask about a regular visitor pass.
  • Poranki: apartments, houses, villas and plots along Bandar Road, with a quieter canal road running alongside. A tutor coming from the city can use the canal road when Bandar Road fills up in the evening.
  • Penamaluru: apartments and independent houses on the Gudivada road, at the outer edge of the Bandar Road belt. A tutor based in Penamaluru, Poranki or Kanuru is the most practical choice for regular classes.

Boards and the school year in Vijayawada

Families in the city follow the Board of Secondary Education, Andhra Pradesh (BSEAP) up to Class 10 and the Board of Intermediate Education, AP (BIEAP) for the two Intermediate years, as well as CBSE and the CISCE's ICSE and ISC, with some needing IB or IGCSE help. When you request a tutor, name the board and your child's medium of study. For Class 10 and the second Intermediate year, start regular lessons early in the session rather than in the last months.

Frequently asked questions

How do home tutors travel in Vijayawada without a metro?

By road, mostly on their own two-wheeler, by auto, by city bus or by car. Tutors who live further out sometimes take a train to Gunadala, Madhura Nagar, Ramavarappadu or Rayanapadu and finish by auto. Picking a tutor from your own zone keeps the trip short and the start time steady.

Which parts of Vijayawada mostly have independent houses?

The northern zone, around Machavaram and Ajit Singh Nagar, and the lanes of One Town have many independent houses, so a tutor usually arrives at the door. Flats are more common around Benz Circle, Patamata and Labbipet, where buildings normally ask visitors to sign in at the gate.

Should we change class timings during Navaratri or the Gunadala festival?

It helps. Roads near the Kanaka Durga temple become very crowded during Navaratri, and the February festival at Gunadala draws large crowds. Families close to either can move a few classes online or shift timings for those days, then return to the usual home slot afterwards.

Can we ask for a tutor who teaches the Intermediate course?

Yes. Name the Board of Intermediate Education, AP, the year and the subjects in your request, along with your child's medium of study. NXTutors then matches two or three tutors who teach that course, and you can check their approach to the syllabus during the free demo.

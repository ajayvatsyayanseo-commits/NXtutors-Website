The capital of Arunachal Pradesh is really two towns joined by one highway. Itanagar, run by the Itanagar Municipal Corporation since 2013, climbs the hillsides around its lettered sectors and markets, while Naharlagun, a foothill town that is administered as part of the same capital complex, spreads out along the rivers further down the road. The capital complex runs from Chandranagar at Itanagar's northern edge to Nirjuli beyond Naharlagun, with Banderdewa on the Assam border and Doimukh nearby.

For home tuition, that shape matters: nearly every journey uses the one highway, and many homes sit inside staff colonies with a gate. This guide covers the four NXTutors zones in the capital region locality by locality, with the details that help a first demo class start on time.

Getting a tutor to your door

Tutors travel by their own two-wheeler, by shared taxi along the highway, or by auto for the last stretch. Three things decide whether a first visit starts on time:

  • The colony gate. Many homes are in official colonies where visitors give a name at the entrance; tell the guard the tutor's name and arrival time in advance.
  • The drop-off point. Give the nearest highway stop or market, such as Ganga Market, Bank Tinali, Zero Point or the Naharlagun daily market, and how to walk or take an auto from there.
  • Highway timing. The highway is busiest when offices and schools open and close, so slots after the evening rush, or on weekend mornings, are easier to keep.

Itanagar North (Chimpu & Ganga)

Government offices, colonies and the busy Ganga Market at the city's northern end. See the Itanagar North zone page.

  • Chimpu: at the northern entry, where the Chimpu and Senki rivers meet. Much of it is offices and staff quarters, so most visits start at a gate.
  • Chandranagar: where the municipal area begins, with a market, several colonies and some light industry on the low ground by the Senki River. The market end of the highway is busy at school closing time, so a late-afternoon or weekend slot works better.
  • Vivek Vihar: a residential area between the highway and the Senki River, with a hilltop part reached by internal roads and the Jollang Road climbing from this side. Hill roads slow down in rain, so keep an online slot as a backup.
  • Ganga Market: one of the main shopping areas, with a shared-taxi counter and an auto stand, and the H, F and G Sectors behind it. A tutor without a vehicle can arrive by shared taxi and walk into the sector.

Central Itanagar

The administrative middle of the city. See the Central Itanagar zone page.

  • Niti Vihar: a largely residential area of government quarters and official bungalows, with private houses on the surrounding slopes and its own small market. Inform the gate when the home is in an official compound.
  • Bank Tinali: a busy junction with several bank branches, flats above shops and quarters in C-II Sector and the Forest Colony. The junction is among the busiest stretches at closing time, so choose a slot after the rush.
  • E-Sector: opposite the Civil Secretariat, with offices, banks and government housing close together. Early evening or weekend classes avoid the office-hour highway.
  • C-Sector: offices, hotels and officers' colonies on the ridge above Gandhi Market, on the highway towards Naharlagun. Parking near the market can be tight, which suits a tutor on a two-wheeler or arriving by shared taxi.
  • Zero Point and P-Sector: a market junction at the eastern end of central Itanagar. A tutor coming from either town can get down at the junction and take an auto for the last stretch.

Naharlagun & Papu Nallah

The stretch of highway between the two towns and the foothill town of Naharlagun, whose railway station opened in 2014 with trains to Guwahati and Delhi. See the Naharlagun & Papu Nallah zone page.

  • Papu Nallah: midway between Itanagar and Naharlagun, with independent houses and small colonies on both sides of the highway. Tutors from Zero Point and Barapani can both cover it.
  • Barapani: the western entry to Naharlagun beside the Pachin River, with a market, offices and housing together. Most homes are a short walk from the market stop; avoid the evening market rush for a first visit.
  • Polo Colony: a residential area on the slopes above the Lagun and Pachin rivers, near Polo Park. Roads rise from the main town, so a tutor on foot may prefer an auto from central Naharlagun or Barapani.
  • Naharlagun: the town centre, with A and B Sectors, Prem Nagar and the daily market, and E, F and G Sectors and G Extension up the hill to the east. Tutors coming by shared taxi often take an auto from the daily market.

Nirjuli, Banderdewa & Doimukh

The smaller towns beyond Naharlagun on NH-415, the highway that starts at Banderdewa and links the capital with Assam. See the Nirjuli, Banderdewa & Doimukh zone page.

  • Nirjuli: a town where the Par and Dikrong rivers flow, combining staff quarters, colonies and independent houses with offices and an industrial area. Staff housing has a gate, so pass on the tutor's name; many tutors living in Naharlagun cover it too.
  • Banderdewa: on the bank of the Dikrong River at the border with Assam, with government staff quarters and independent houses. As a smaller town, many families pair a home tutor for core subjects with online classes for the rest.
  • Doimukh: a small town and a separate administrative circle of Papum Pare district. Local home tutors are fewer here, so a regular weekly slot with a tutor from a nearby town, plus online classes, is the usual pattern.

Boards and the school year

CBSE lists the government schools of Arunachal Pradesh among its affiliated schools and conducts the Class X and Class XII examinations for them, and school education in the state follows the 10+2 structure. Give the board, class and, for Classes 11–12, the stream in your request; an online tutor can cover any other board no nearby tutor teaches. See CBSE home tutors in Itanagar and the official CBSE website for current dates and patterns.

In the monsoon, hill roads in both towns slow down on very wet days. Allow extra time, and agree with the tutor at the start which classes move online when rain is heavy.

Frequently asked questions

Do tutors cover both Itanagar and Naharlagun?

Many do, because one highway links the two towns. Tutors living near Zero Point, Papu Nallah or Barapani sit between them and can often cover either side. For a regular slot, a tutor from your own zone is usually the steadier choice, with online classes for any subject no nearby tutor teaches.

Our home is in a government staff colony. What should we do before the demo?

Tell the gate the tutor's name and arrival time in advance, and share the colony name, the quarter or block number and a landmark with the tutor. Mention the nearest highway stop as well, so a tutor arriving by shared taxi or auto knows where to get down.

Which localities are easiest for tutors to reach?

Those on or near the highway with a market stop, such as Ganga Market, Bank Tinali, E-Sector, Zero Point and central Naharlagun. Hilltop parts of Vivek Vihar, Niti Vihar or Polo Colony take a little longer, so a clear landmark and a fixed weekly slot help.

We live in Nirjuli or Doimukh. Can we still get a home tutor?

Yes, though there are fewer local tutors than in the twin towns. NXTutors looks for the nearest tutors first, which often means someone living in Naharlagun. Many families in the smaller towns combine home visits for the core subjects with online classes for the rest of the week.

What happens to classes on very rainy days?

Hill roads slow down in heavy monsoon rain, so allow extra time or switch that day's class online. Agree the rule at the start: which classes move online, how much notice each side gives, and whether an online class carries the same rate as a home visit. That keeps wet evenings simple and fair.

ICSE Class 10 is a long examination season at the end of a long year. A student writes English twice, science three times and History, Civics and Geography twice, alongside Mathematics, a second language and the remaining Group II and Group III subjects, and the internal marks for most of those papers are earned in the months before. In Kolkata the year also has the autumn Puja holidays in the middle of it, which can either be a rest that costs a month or a revision window that saves one.

This plan follows the ICSE year from the start of the session to the result, month by month, using only what the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations publishes on cisce.org: the regulations, the subject syllabuses, the 2026 examination timetable and the Analysis of Pupil Performance reports. Your school's own dates will differ by a few weeks, so write them into the plan as soon as they are announced.

What the CISCE rules fix before the year begins

  • Subjects are already locked. Candidates are registered in Class 9, subjects cannot change after 15 September of the Class 9 year, and a Class 10 subject must have been studied in Class 9. The Class 10 year is about those subjects only.
  • Attendance counts. First-time candidates need 75% attendance in each year of the two-year course, computed up to 15 February. Long breaks in winter can matter more than families expect.
  • The pass standard. The pass mark in each subject is 33%, and a pass certificate needs passes in at least five subjects including English, plus a pass grade in Socially Useful Productive Work and Community Service, assessed by the school.
  • Internal assessment is part of the result. Groups I and II are 80% paper and 20% internal; Group III subjects are half paper, half internal.

In 2026 the papers ran from 17 February to 30 March. English Language came first, Mathematics on 2 March, the three sciences on 9, 11 and 13 March, and History and Civics and Geography on 16 and 18 March, with the remaining electives at the end. Fifteen minutes of reading time comes before each paper, and candidates are seated half an hour before the start. The order matters for planning: with English early and the sciences close together, there is little room to revise a whole science between papers.

Subject by subject

Mathematics

The paper rewards complete working. The 2026 specimen paper had a compulsory Section A of 40 marks, including multiple-choice items, and a Section B of 40 marks in which candidates answer any four questions. Commercial mathematics, such as GST, banking and shares, sits beside algebra, geometry, trigonometry and statistics, and CISCE's October 2025 examiners' report listed GST, recurring deposits and shares among the topics candidates found difficult. Keep the two internal assignments on schedule; an external examiner marks half of them. Our ICSE maths tutors in Kolkata page covers the paper chapter by chapter.

Physics, Chemistry and Biology

Three two-hour papers inside one week leave no room for a weak science. Physics needs numericals with units, Chemistry needs balanced equations and exact observations, and Biology needs labelled diagrams and precise terms. Each carries 20 marks of practical work, so the practical file is part of the result, not a formality.

English and History, Civics and Geography

These are the papers where knowledge is often sound but answers run short. Read the literature texts twice early in the year, practise composition and letter formats against the word limits, and do map work weekly rather than in March.

Second language and Group III

Bengali, Hindi or the other second language gets the least time in many homes; a fixed weekly slot fixes that. Group III subjects carry half their marks internally, so a slack term costs more there than anywhere else.

Using CISCE's own documents

  • Syllabus for the exam year. Use the one for your child's year of examination, not an older copy.
  • Specimen papers. The surest guide to the current format, including the mix of multiple-choice and long questions.
  • Analysis of Pupil Performance. CISCE publishes these subject by subject after each exam, with examiners' notes on which topics were found difficult and what a good answer included. Read the latest one with your child in December.
  • Past papers. Useful for practice, but check them against the current syllabus before relying on a pattern.

After the papers: result, recheck and improvement

The 2026 timetable said results would be declared in April to May 2026, online on the CISCE website. A candidate unhappy with a result can ask for a recheck within four days of the result, and after the recheck result can apply for re-evaluation within three days. CISCE also runs an improvement examination in the same year: any candidate who sat the main examination can take it in up to three subjects, writing the theory paper again while project marks carry forward, and the higher of the two marks counts. Schools register candidates for it on dates CISCE notifies.

A parent's checklist for the board year

  • Put the school's test, pre-board, practical and project dates on one calendar in April.
  • Ask in July and again in November whether maths assignments and science practical files are complete.
  • Look at the scripts, not just the marks, after every school exam; the mistake pattern tells you whether the gap is concept, speed or presentation.
  • Keep attendance safe until mid-February.
  • Download the CISCE timetable the day it appears and plan revision in its order.

Frequently asked questions

When will the ICSE 2027 timetable be published?

CISCE publishes it on cisce.org and sends it to schools; the regulations say only that it is made available well before the examination. The 2026 timetable was dated 13 November 2025, with papers from 17 February to 30 March 2026. Plan revision once the 2027 timetable is out.

How are the 20 internal marks in ICSE Maths awarded?

Through at least two assignments set during the year. Each is marked independently by the subject teacher and by an external examiner, for ten marks each. The assignments must be the student's own work, so a tutor can explain methods but should never write them.

What is the pass standard for ICSE?

The pass mark in each subject is 33%. To receive a pass certificate a candidate needs passes in at least five subjects including English, and a pass grade in Socially Useful Productive Work and Community Service, which the school assesses. Results are shown as grades from 1 to 9.

Can my child improve a weak ICSE result in the same year?

Yes. Any candidate who sat the main examination can take CISCE's improvement examination in up to three subjects. The theory paper is written again, project marks carry forward, and the higher of the two marks counts. Schools register candidates on dates that CISCE notifies.

How should we use the Puja holidays in Class 10?

As a revision window rather than a full break. An hour or so of written work a day, on the weakest chapters from the half-yearly, keeps momentum for the November syllabus finish and December pre-boards. If the tutor cannot travel that week, a few online sessions keep the routine.

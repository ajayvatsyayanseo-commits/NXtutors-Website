You will get a short explanation of each place we use AI (matching, the NXT AI assistant, TutorTwin and the WhatsApp hand-off), a clear account of where AI stops and people take over, what information we use and why, and practical advice on how to let your child use AI without it doing their thinking for them. Our approach is simple to state: AI-first tutoring, with real teachers.

1. Matching: a shortlist instead of a long directory

When you look for a tutor, you tell us four things: the subject, the class, the board and your area. You can do this through the search on the website or on WhatsApp. Instead of showing you hundreds of profiles to scroll through, we shortlist two or three tutors who fit.

The matching looks at:

  • Subject – for example, Class 11 chemistry rather than "science".
  • Board – CBSE, ICSE, ISC, IB or IGCSE, because each board asks for different things in the exam.
  • Class or exam – school marks, board exams, or JEE/NEET preparation.
  • Distance or online – for home tuition, how far the tutor is from your area; otherwise, whether the class can happen online.

Verified tutors are put first. Some profiles on the site are sample profiles, and they are clearly marked as samples, so you always know which tutors have been checked.

What to do: be specific when you search. "Class 9 ICSE maths, home tuition, near Sector 56" gives a far better shortlist than "maths tutor". If you want to browse first, you can find a tutor yourself, or start with a local page such as Maths home tutors in Gurgaon.

3. TutorTwin: homework help on WhatsApp

TutorTwin is an AI tutor made by NXTutors Edtech that works inside WhatsApp, with no separate app. A student can send a typed question, a photo of homework, a PDF or a voice note, and gets back a step-by-step explanation, usually within seconds, at any hour. It replies in English, Hindi or Hinglish, depending on how the student writes.

It keeps a small memory of what the student finds difficult, so it can set practice questions like ones they got wrong. It is an AI and says so; it never claims to be a person or a teacher. It is also a tutor, not a ghost-writer: it will not do assessed work as if it were the student's own.

Where AI stops and people start

AI helps with speed. The decisions that matter for your child are made by people.

  • People verify tutors. A tutor is marked verified only after a person has checked them. Software does not hand out that label.
  • The demo class decides. The first class is a free demo. A shortlist is only a starting point; you and your child judge the fit in a real lesson. If the fit is not right, switching tutor is free. Our demo class checklist explains what to look for.
  • Teachers set the plan. Which chapters to fix first, how much practice to give, when to move on – these are the tutor's calls, made after they have seen how your child works.
  • AI can make mistakes. Both NXT AI and TutorTwin can be wrong. Important answers, especially in maths and science, should be checked against the textbook or with the tutor.

What information we use, and why

We use the details you choose to share with us: typically your child's subject, class, board, area and any questions you ask. We use them for two purposes. First, to match your child with suitable tutors. Second, to continue the conversation, so that when you move to WhatsApp or speak to the team, you do not have to start from the beginning.

A few sensible habits help:

  • Share what is needed to find a tutor. You do not need to give more personal detail than that in a chat.
  • If your child is under 18 and will use TutorTwin, a parent or guardian should set up or permit the subscription.
  • If something you shared is wrong or out of date, tell the team so the match is based on the right information.

How to use AI sensibly with your child

AI can help between classes, or it can let a child copy answers without learning. The difference lies in the habits you set at home.

  1. Attempt first, ask second. Ask your child to try a question for a fixed time, say ten minutes, before asking an AI. Struggle is where learning happens.
  2. Ask for a hint, not the answer. Encourage your child to ask "what is the first step?" rather than "solve this". Then they finish it themselves.
  3. Explain it back. After reading a worked solution, your child should close the chat and explain the method to you in their own words. If they cannot, they have not learnt it yet.
  4. Check important answers. Compare with the textbook, the answer key or the tutor. AI can be confidently wrong.
  5. Never for assessed work. Projects, internal assessments and anything that will be marked as the student's own work must be the student's own.
  6. Tell the tutor. If your child keeps getting stuck on the same kind of question, that is a signal for the next lesson.

Frequently asked questions

Does an AI choose my child's tutor?

AI helps shortlist two or three tutors based on subject, board, class or exam, and area or online. You make the choice, and the free demo class is where you and your child decide whether the fit is right.

Are all the tutors on NXTutors verified?

Verified tutors have been checked by a person and are shown first. The site also has sample profiles, which are clearly marked as samples, so you can tell the difference.

Can I trust what NXT AI tells me about fees?

Use it as a guide. NXT AI can make mistakes, so confirm fees, timings and any booking details with the NXTutors team before you pay. As a rough range, home tuition on NXTutors is about ₹800–₹2,500 per hour.

Is TutorTwin a replacement for a tutor?

No. TutorTwin helps between lessons with doubts and revision. A human tutor plans the learning and adjusts the approach. Many families use both.

What happens if the tutor is not a good fit?

Tell the team, ideally with a line or two on what did not work, such as pace, explanation style or timing. Switching tutor is free, and the team can use your feedback to suggest a better match.

What goes into a tutor's quote?

Four things move a quote more than anything else. Mention each in your request so matched tutors can quote for the real job:

  • The class. A Class 3 reader and a Class 12 physics student need very different preparation.
  • The subject mix. One tutor for maths and science together is a different request from three separate tutors for physics, chemistry and maths in Class 11.
  • The goal. Keeping pace with school, preparing for board papers and preparing for JEE or NEET alongside the board syllabus are three distinct pieces of work.
  • The length of each class. A longer class at the same hourly rate costs more per visit, so say what length you have in mind.

Five questions that settle the weekly hours

The number of hours you book shapes the monthly bill far more than a small gap between two hourly rates. Before you compare tutors, answer these at home:

  1. What exactly is going wrong? Homework that never gets finished calls for short, regular visits. Weak marks in one chapter may need only a few focused weeks.
  2. How many subjects need help? List them separately. A younger child may manage with one tutor for everything; a senior student often needs a specialist per subject.
  3. How long can the child concentrate? Primary pupils tire quickly, so two short visits usually beat one long one. Older students solving long problems may prefer a longer sitting.
  4. Is a board year coming? Class 10 and Class 12 usually need more hours in the final months than at the start of the session.
  5. When will you review the plan? Pick a unit test or term end at which you and the tutor decide to keep, cut or stop the hours.

How does a CBSE board year change the plan?

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) lists the government schools of Arunachal Pradesh among its affiliated schools, and it conducts the Class X and Class XII examinations for the schools affiliated to it. School education in the state follows the uniform 10+2 structure, so the two board years fall at the end of Class 10 and Class 12. For the current pattern and dates, use the board's official website.

A board year rarely changes the hourly rate. It changes how the hours are spread:

Which subjects suit online classes?

Many Itanagar families pair a home tutor for core subjects with online classes for the rest. Online classes tend to work well for:

  • Specialist senior subjects where few local tutors teach the exact syllabus
  • Entrance preparation that relies on shared screens, past papers and timed tests
  • Days of heavy monsoon rain, when the hill roads are slow for a tutor on a two-wheeler
  • Homes in the smaller towns of the region, where home tutors are fewer

One tutor may quote per hour, another per month, a third per class. Put both quotes into the same shape first:

Once both quotes answer the same six points, the comparison rests on what each tutor will do, not on a headline number.

What to put in writing

A short message that the tutor confirms is enough. Cover how the fee is counted, the days and times, the address with a landmark and the nearest drop-off point, how much notice either side gives for a cancellation, when a missed class is made up, which days move online, and the date you will review progress together. In the official staff colonies, also note that you will give the tutor's name at the gate in advance, so the first visit starts on time.

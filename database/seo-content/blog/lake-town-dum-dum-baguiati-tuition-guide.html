North-east Kolkata is held together by two roads. VIP Road, finished in 1962, runs from Ultadanga to the airport past Lake Town, Bangur Avenue, Dum Dum Park, Kestopur, Baguiati, Teghoria and Kaikhali. Jessore Road runs through Dum Dum and along the northern side of Lake Town and Bangur Avenue, and as Belgachia Road it reaches into north Kolkata. Between and around them sit some of the easiest homes in the city for a visiting tutor to reach, and a few of the hardest, sometimes only a short distance apart.

This guide is for families in that belt who want a home tutor. It goes locality by locality through Lake Town, Bangur Avenue, Kestopur, Baguiati and Dum Dum, then the northern edge at Belgachia and Sinthee, and shows how the way a tutor arrives should shape the address you send, the time you fix and the mix of home and online lessons. NXTutors groups the first five as one zone, Lake Town, Dum Dum and Baguiati.

Two councils, three rail systems, one main road

Lake Town, Bangur Avenue and Nagerbazar fall under South Dum Dum Municipality. Kestopur and Baguiati belong to the Bidhannagar Municipal Corporation, formed in 2015 from the Bidhannagar and Rajarhat-Gopalpur areas. Dum Dum as a whole is divided between the Dum Dum, North Dum Dum and South Dum Dum municipalities. For a family this matters little, but it explains why addresses on either side of VIP Road can look quite different.

For a tutor, rail access matters far more:

  • Blue Line metro: Dum Dum and Belgachia stations both opened on 12 November 1984.
  • Suburban railway: Dum Dum Junction on the Sealdah–Ranaghat line, where the Circular Railway also begins; Dum Dum Cantonment on the Sealdah–Bangaon line; Bidhannagar Road and Patipukur for the Lake Town side.
  • Yellow Line metro: running since 22 August 2025 from Noapara through Dum Dum Cantonment along Jessore Road to the airport station; its Birati section is still under construction.

Baguiati has no metro station of its own yet, which is the single biggest difference inside the zone.

Lake Town and Bangur Avenue

Lake Town lies between VIP Road and Jessore Road, with Bangur Avenue, Dum Dum Park, Nagerbazar, Dakshindari and the Kalindi housing estate around it. The Ultadanga flyover, opened in 2011, brings traffic from the EM Bypass onto VIP Road here. Bangur Avenue has Jessore Road on its northern edge and VIP Road on its southern one, and shares a postal code with Lake Town, Dum Dum Park and Shyamnagar. A tutor coming by train usually finishes with a short auto or bus ride, so an early-evening slot is often realistic.

Kestopur

Kestopur has VIP Road on its western boundary and Salt Lake close by. Since 2022 the Salt Lake–Kestopur bridge has linked the two directly, which brings tutors who live in Salt Lake Sector II or work near Sector V within easy reach. Inside the locality, Kestopur Main Road runs from Belgachia Road through Dum Dum Park, with Canal Side Road parallel to it, so naming which of the two your building is on saves a first-time tutor a wrong turn. A tutor on a two-wheeler often has the simplest trip here.

Baguiati

Baguiati takes in sub-localities including Ashwini Nagar, Jyangra, Raghunathpur and Arjunpur, with Baguiati Bazar as the local market and Teghoria and the Rajarhat side as neighbours. A flyover from Dum Dum Park to Raghunathpur, opened in March 2015, carries through traffic above VIP Road. With no metro stop, most tutors finish by bus or auto along VIP Road, so the pool leans towards tutors who already live nearby, in Baguiati itself, Rajarhat or Kestopur.

Dum Dum

Dum Dum, including Dum Dum Cantonment, Gora Bazar and the Nagerbazar market crossing, is the transport hub of the whole belt. Four rail services meet here, so a family in Dum Dum can draw on tutors from much of north and central Kolkata.

The northern edge: Belgachia and Sinthee

Two neighbourhoods just beyond the zone share its roads and stations. Belgachia sits on Belgachia Road, part of Jessore Road, with ready two-bedroom flats beside older houses, and its Blue Line station has been open since 1984. Sinthee, around Sinthee More on Barrackpore Trunk Road, is mostly apartments, with Dum Dum metro and Dum Dum Junction as its nearest rail links. NXTutors places both in the North Kolkata zone, but a tutor who serves Dum Dum can often serve them too.

The directions note: what to send before the demo

Addresses in this belt range from Lake Town's planned streets to Baguiati's sub-localities, so one short message before the first class saves most of the confusion.

  • Locality and sub-locality: for example Baguiati, then Jyangra or Arjunpur; Kestopur, then Main Road or Canal Side Road.
  • Nearest station and the onward hop: "Dum Dum metro, then an auto towards Nagerbazar" tells a tutor more than a postcode.
  • A landmark off VIP Road or Jessore Road: a bus stop, a market or a park.
  • Building and floor: and whether there is a gate desk that needs the tutor's name and phone number in advance.
  • Where to leave a two-wheeler: useful in Kestopur and Baguiati if the tutor rides.

Home, online or both?

The pattern that suits a family here usually follows the rail map:

  • Dum Dum, Lake Town, Bangur Avenue: with good rail links, home lessons in most subjects are practical, including a specialist for a Class 11–12 science or for WBJEE.
  • Baguiati and the far side of Kestopur: a nearby tutor for the main subject at home, and an online tutor for a specialist subject that no nearby tutor covers.
  • Younger children anywhere in the belt: home lessons, kept short and regular, with a tutor who lives close enough to come twice or three times a week.

Whatever the board, say so in the request. Schools across Kolkata follow the West Bengal boards, CBSE and ICSE, and a Madhyamik or Higher Secondary tutor is a different match from an ICSE tutor. Frequently asked questions

Which stations should a tutor use for Lake Town and Bangur Avenue?

Bidhannagar Road or Dum Dum Junction on the suburban railway, Patipukur for parts of Bangur Avenue, or the Blue Line to Belgachia or Dum Dum. From there an auto or bus along VIP Road or Jessore Road covers the last stretch. Name the station and the onward route in your directions note.

Is it harder to find a home tutor in Baguiati?

The pool is narrower, because Baguiati has no metro station yet and tutors come by bus, auto or two-wheeler along VIP Road. Tutors who live in Baguiati, Kestopur or Rajarhat are the easiest match, and an online tutor can cover a specialist subject that no nearby tutor teaches.

Does the Yellow Line help families in Dum Dum?

Yes, on the Dum Dum Cantonment side. Since 22 August 2025 it has run from Noapara through Dum Dum Cantonment along Jessore Road to the airport, adding a fourth rail service to the area. Its Birati section is still under construction, so check your nearest working stop before relying on it.

Can a tutor from Salt Lake reach Kestopur easily?

Usually, yes. The Salt Lake–Kestopur bridge, open since 2022, links the two directly, and Karunamoyee and Salt Lake Sector V on the Green Line are the nearest metro stations. Tell the tutor whether you are on Kestopur Main Road or Canal Side Road to save time on the first visit.

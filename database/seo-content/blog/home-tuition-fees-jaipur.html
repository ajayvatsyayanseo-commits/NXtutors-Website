Step 2: how do we read two or three fees side by side?

Every fee on your NXTutors shortlist is visible before the demo, so the comparison happens before anyone travels to your home. The hourly rate is only one line. Before the demo, note for each tutor:

  • Class length: an hour and a half twice a week and one hour three times a week give the same weekly hours but a different number of trips.

Step 4: does your part of Jaipur change the sums?

Not the band. Location mostly sets the size of the shortlist: the more tutors who can get to you without trouble, the more fees there are to weigh. Jaipur has one working metro route, the Pink Line, which runs from Mansarovar along New Sanganer Road and past Civil Lines, the railway station and Sindhi Camp to Chandpole, with its underground stretch under the old city to Badi Chaupar. The Orange Line, a north-south route, is planned and under construction, so for now it does not help any trip. In the south, railway stations at Gandhinagar, Durgapura, Sanganer and Getor Jagatpura fill some of the gaps.

Step 5: how should hours change through the school year?

A budget that stays flat for twelve months usually pays for hours nobody needs in one term and runs short in another. A simple rhythm for a Jaipur family:

  1. April to the summer break: for CBSE and CISCE schools the new session starts in April. Two hours a week per weak subject is often enough to set the foundations for the year.
  2. Before the half-yearly exams: add an hour a week for a few weeks if a unit test shows a gap, then drop back.
  3. Winter and the pre-boards: for Class 10 and Class 12, this is where the extra hours earn their place. Plan the increase in advance so it does not come as a surprise.
  4. After the exams: pause, or keep one class a week to prepare for the next class.

Step 6: one tutor, two tutors or a mix?

In the junior and middle classes, one tutor for maths and science is common and keeps the number of trips down. From Class 11, physics, chemistry and maths often need separate tutors, each with their own fee. A mix can stretch the budget: book the home visit for whichever subject your child finds hardest to follow on paper, and move a second subject, or an IB or IGCSE specialist, to a screen. What should we agree before the first paid class?

  • What happens when either side cancels a class.
  • The rate for any extra classes added in the pre-board weeks.
  • When fees are paid: at the start of each month, or once the month's classes are done.
  • How progress will be checked, for example against the next unit test.

How do we estimate a monthly tuition budget?

Multiply the weekly hours by four, then by the hourly fee. Two hours a week is eight hours in a four-week month: ₹6,400 at ₹800 an hour and ₹20,000 at ₹2,500 an hour. Add each subject separately, and plan extra hours for the pre-board months in Class 10 and Class 12.

Is home tuition dearer in C-Scheme than in Mansarovar or Jagatpura?

No. One band covers the whole city, and the student's class, board and subjects, together with the tutor's experience, do most of the work in setting a fee. What your area changes is the number of tutors within easy reach, so a well-connected home simply sees more fees side by side.

Do RBSE students pay less than CBSE or ICSE students?

Not as a rule. The board is one factor, but the class, the subject and the goal matter more, and every tutor names their own rate. What does matter is choosing a tutor who already teaches your board's books, so fewer paid hours go on settling in during the first weeks.

Does living near a Pink Line station help?

It can widen the shortlist. A tutor riding the Pink Line can reach homes near stations such as Mansarovar, Shyam Nagar, Civil Lines or Sindhi Camp from further along the line. It does not lower the fee itself, but more tutors within reach means more fees to compare before the free demo.

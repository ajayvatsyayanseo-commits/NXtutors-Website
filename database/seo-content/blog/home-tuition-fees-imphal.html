What makes one quote differ from another?

Two tutors can quote different rates for the same child, and neither is wrong. Before comparing numbers, check that both are pricing the same job. These are the points that usually explain the gap:

  • The class and the stakes. A Class 4 reading habit and a Class 12 physics paper are different pieces of work, and senior classes sit toward the upper end of the range.
  • Subject load. One tutor for mathematics alone is a different request from one tutor covering physics, chemistry and mathematics together.
  • Entrance work. If JEE or NEET practice runs alongside the board syllabus, the tutor needs experience of both, and quotes reflect that.
  • Board or programme. State board, CBSE, ICSE/ISC, IB or IGCSE each bring their own material; IB and IGCSE specialists usually charge more.
  • Length of each visit. An hourly rate across a longer class is simply a larger amount each time the tutor comes.

Put all five in your request, and the quotes you receive will be easier to compare like for like. The NXTutors pricing guide explains each factor further.

Manipur's three exam stages and what they do to your hours

State board students in Imphal meet two examining bodies. The Board of Secondary Education, Manipur (BOSEM) conducts the High School Leaving Certificate (HSLC) examination at the end of Class 10 and publishes its results on bosem.in. The Council of Higher Secondary Education, Manipur (COHSEM), set up in 1992, conducts both a Class XI examination and the Higher Secondary Examination after Class 12. Its site, cohsem.nic.in, carries the syllabus for Classes XI–XII, an academic calendar, internal assessment guidelines and previous question papers. Check dates and patterns there, not from memory.

That structure matters for planning because Class 11 is not a quiet year in Manipur. Families who budget only for Class 10 and Class 12 are often caught out.

A simple way to settle the weekly hours

Rather than starting from a budget, start from the problem. Work through these steps with your child before you send a request:

  1. Name the subjects that are actually slipping. Use the last two school tests, not a general feeling.
  2. Decide what the tutor is for. Homework support, filling old gaps and exam practice need different amounts of time.
  3. Pick a starting pattern. Primary children usually manage short, frequent visits; senior students often prefer fewer, longer sittings for long problems.
  4. Set a check-in point. A unit test or the end of a term is a natural moment to add, trim or stop hours.
  5. Write the class length as times. "Five to six" is clearer than "about an hour", and it keeps the bill predictable.

Most families find that a fixed number of hours, reviewed each term, controls cost better than hunting for a slightly lower rate.

Home visits, online classes or a mix?

An online class carries no travel, and it opens the choice to tutors anywhere in India. In Imphal, a mixed plan often makes sense:

  • a home tutor for the subjects that need close supervision, such as primary maths or Class 10 science;
  • an online tutor for Imphal students for a specialist need, such as IB, IGCSE or an advanced entrance topic;
  • an agreed switch to an online session on evenings of heavy rain, so the routine holds through the monsoon.

Five fee questions to settle at the demo

The free demo is mostly about teaching, but the end of the visit is the right time to clear up money matters face to face:

  1. Is the fee charged per hour, per visit or per month, and if monthly, how many classes does that cover?
  2. What notice does each side give for a cancelled class, and how is a missed class made up?
  3. On a day of very heavy rain, does the class move online, and at what rate?
  4. Before the HSLC, Class XI or Higher Secondary papers, how many extra revision classes are likely, and are they at the usual rate?
  5. When shall we review the plan together: after a unit test, or at the end of a term?

Ask the tutor to confirm the answers in a short message. That note settles most disagreements before they begin. Our demo class checklist for parents covers the teaching side of the same visit.

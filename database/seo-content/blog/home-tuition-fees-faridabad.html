How do we turn one fee range into a budget?

Treat each subject as its own budget line. For every line, decide the weekly hours, multiply by four for a month, and price the result at both ends of the band. Then add the lines. Working per subject makes it easy to see where the money goes, and to change one line without redoing the whole plan.

How should the budget change through the school year?

A tuition budget is not flat. Most schools start the session in April, and the load rises as exams approach. Planning by season keeps the exam months from being a surprise.

SeasonWhat is going onBudget move
April to JuneNew session, new syllabus and often new teachersSet the baseline hours for each line; use the free demo to test fit before committing
July to SeptemberThe main teaching term, with the first unit testsHold the baseline; review each line once the first test results are in
October to DecemberSecond term; for Classes 10 and 12, revision for pre-boards begins in many schoolsFor board classes, consider an extra hour a week in the weakest subject; keep junior plans steady
January to MarchSchool finals, and board exams for Classes 10 and 12 (check the board's official date sheet)Short bursts of extra hours before each paper, then a planned pause once exams end

One way to handle the uneven months is to set aside a small amount each month from April, so the extra hours before exams are already paid for when they come.

What moves a tutor's quote within the band?

  • Class and board: junior classes sit lower; Classes 11–12, IB, IGCSE, JEE and NEET sit toward the upper end.
  • Subject: senior physics, chemistry and maths usually need a separate tutor each, and so a separate line.
  • Experience: a long record with your board and class is worth more to most families than a lower rate.
  • The trip: a tutor who lives nearby, or can come by metro, has less travel to build into a quote.
  • Class length: fewer, longer classes can mean fewer trips for the same weekly hours.
  • Format: one subject at home and another online can widen the choice of tutors.

How does your part of Faridabad shape the plan?

Location rarely sets the fee on its own, but it decides how many tutors can reach you easily and how smoothly each visit starts. More tutors within reach means more quotes to compare.

Along the Violet Line

NIT, Old Faridabad, the central sectors and Sectors 28 to 31 and 37 sit on or near Mathura Road, where the Violet Line section from Badarpur Border to Escorts Mujesar opened on 6 September 2015. Sectors such as Sector 15A, Sector 19 and Sector 28 have a station inside or next door, so tutors from across the city can come by metro. Our NIT and central Faridabad guide maps each sector to its station.

Across the Agra canal

Greater Faridabad has no metro station of its own, and most homes are in gated societies, such as those in Sector 81 or Sector 85. A tutor who lives in Neharpar, or rides in from a sector near the canal crossings, keeps the trip simple, and a standing visitor pass saves time at the gate on every visit. In the plotted blocks of Sector 84 the tutor comes to the door. See the Greater Faridabad (Neharpar) guide.

Ballabhgarh and the Aravalli side

Ballabhgarh has the Violet Line terminus at Raja Nahar Singh, reached by the extension that opened on 19 November 2018, beside Ballabhgarh railway station, and most of its sectors are plotted. Around Sainik Colony and Sector 46, stations are further off and tutors usually come by auto or their own vehicle, so the class time matters more. The Ballabhgarh and Surajkund guide has the detail.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a home tutor cost in Faridabad?

Across NXTutors, most home-tuition sessions fall between ₹800 and ₹2,500 an hour; Classes 11–12, IB/IGCSE and JEE/NEET sit toward the upper end; specialists for IB HL or JEE Advanced can charge more. Each tutor sets their own fee, and you see it before the free demo class.

How much should we budget for Class 12 science?

Plan each subject as its own line. Two hours a week each of physics, chemistry and maths is twenty-four hours a month: ₹19,200 at ₹800 an hour and ₹60,000 at ₹2,500. Class 12 sits toward the upper end of the band, so plan nearer the higher figure.

Does living near a Violet Line station change the fee?

Not directly, since class, board, subject and experience set most of a fee. A home near a station can be reached by more tutors who travel by metro, so you may get more quotes to compare. In Greater Faridabad, with no station, a tutor from a nearby sector keeps things simple.

Why should the budget change during the year?

The load is uneven. Most schools start the session in April, the main term runs steadily, and extra hours usually cluster before school finals and, for Classes 10 and 12, board exams. Setting aside a little each month from April means those exam-time hours are already covered.

Can we save by mixing home and online classes?

Often, yes. Keep a home tutor for the subject that needs the most hands-on help and take a second subject or a specialist board online. That cuts the number of trips a week and widens the choice of tutors. Switching tutor later is free on NXTutors if the mix does not suit.

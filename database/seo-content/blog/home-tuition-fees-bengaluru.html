What should we settle before the first paid class?

Compare quotes by the monthly total for the same plan, not the hourly rate alone, then agree these five points:

  1. Length and number of sessions. Write down the weekly plan so the monthly total is clear.
  2. Cancellations. What happens when the family cancels, and what happens when the tutor does?
  3. Exam season. Are extra sessions before the SSLC, PUC, board or school exams charged at the same rate?
  4. Payment timing. Monthly in advance, or after the sessions are held?
  5. Arrival routine. In a gated community, who registers the tutor at the gate each week, so paid time is not lost at the entrance?

Frequently asked questions

How much does a home tutor cost in Bengaluru?

Across NXTutors, most home-tuition sessions fall between ₹800 and ₹2,500 an hour; Classes 11–12, IB/IGCSE and JEE/NEET sit toward the upper end; specialists for IB HL or JEE Advanced can charge more. Each tutor sets their own fee, and you see it on your shortlist before the free demo.

Is tuition for the Karnataka state board cheaper than for CBSE or ICSE?

The board alone does not set the fee. The class, the subject, any entrance goal and the tutor's experience with your syllabus matter more, and each tutor sets their own rate. What the board does decide is which tutors fit, so ask whether they have taught the state textbooks in your child's medium.

How many sessions a month should we budget for?

Two one-hour sessions a week in one subject means eight sessions in a four-week month: ₹6,400 at ₹800 an hour and ₹20,000 at ₹2,500. Add a line for each extra subject, and keep a separate amount for the extra sessions many students need in the weeks before exams.

Will a new metro line near us lower the fee?

Not directly. An open station lets tutors from further along the line reach you, which usually means more quotes to compare. The Pink and Blue lines are not open yet, so plan on the Purple, Green and Yellow lines and today's road routes, and fix a slot away from office traffic.

Is online tuition cheaper than home tuition in Bengaluru?

Sometimes, but not always. Each tutor sets a fee for each format, so ask both questions. Online classes remove the trip and widen the pool of specialists, which helps for IB, IGCSE or senior science. Many families keep one subject at home and take a second online to balance cost and contact.

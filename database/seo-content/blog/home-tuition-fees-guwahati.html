Is a state board tutor cheaper than a CBSE or ICSE tutor?

Not as a rule. Assam's state board, long run as SEBA for Class 10 and AHSEC for the higher secondary years and since brought together under one state school education board, does not come with a lower fee attached. What matters is fit: a state board student needs a tutor who teaches from the prescribed textbooks and in the medium the school uses, and a CBSE or ICSE student needs someone who knows that board's papers. Pick the tutor who fits first, then compare fees among those who do. Syllabus and exam details should always come from the board's own notices.

Five ways Guwahati families keep the total in check

  1. Work around coaching, not against it. Around Chandmari and GS Road, many students already attend evening coaching. Decide what the tutor is for, such as clearing unsolved sheets or the board syllabus, so you do not pay twice for the same hour.
  2. Choose one tutor for two subjects only where it fits. Maths and science up to Class 10 often sit well with one person; in Classes 11 and 12, a specialist per subject usually does more for the same hours.
  3. Put the specialist paper online. A nearby home tutor for everyday subjects plus an online tutor for ICSE literature, IB or a senior specialist paper saves cross-city trips.
  4. Agree what happens in festival weeks. Bohag Bihu in April and Durga Puja in the autumn break the routine. Settle in advance whether missed classes are moved, taught online or simply not charged.
  5. Review after eight weeks. If the gap is closing, reduce the hours; if not, discuss the plan with the tutor before adding more.

What should we agree before the first paid class?

  • The fee per hour, the length of each class and how many classes a week.
  • Whether payment is monthly in advance or after the classes are held.
  • What happens when either side cancels, including on heavy-rain days in the monsoon.
  • Whether extra classes before exams are charged at the same rate.
  • For apartment buildings, who tells the gate about the tutor, so no paid time is lost at the entrance.
